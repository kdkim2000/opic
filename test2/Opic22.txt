Opic22 — 문제 스크립트 (여우 MOCK TEST 22, 난이도 5~6)

[Background Survey]
1. 일 경험 없음
2. 학생 - 아니오, 수업 등록 후 5년
3. 독신자로서 개인 주택이나 아파트에 거주
4. (총 8개) 영화보기, 콘서트 가기, 공연/연극 관람하기, 공원 가기, 해변가기, 카페/커피전문점 가기
5. (총 1개) 음악감상
6. (총 3개) 조깅, 걷기, 신체 운동을 즐기지 않음
7. (총 2개) 국내여행, 해외여행

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[약속 (돌발)]
Q2. What kinds of appointments do you usually make, and how do you arrange or schedule them?

Q3. Can you tell me about an appointment you had when you were a child? Maybe it was a doctor's or dentist's appointment. Tell me about your experience with an appointment from your childhood.

Q4. Do you have a memorable experience related to an appointment? Was it interesting or surprising? What happened, and what made it so memorable for you?

------------------------------------------------------------
[가족·친구 (돌발)]
Q5. Can you tell me about one of your friends or a family member? What is he or she like?

Q6. What do you usually do when you spend time with your friends or family? What activities do you like to do with them?

Q7. Can you describe the last time you spent time with a friend or family member? When was it? Who did you meet, and what did you do?

------------------------------------------------------------
[공원 가기]
Q8. You indicated in the survey that you go to parks. Tell me about a park you like to visit. What is it like? What do people do at the park?

Q9. When did you first start visiting parks? How did you become interested in going to parks? What made you go?

Q10. Can you tell me about a memorable experience you had at a park? Was it interesting? Tell me what happened in detail.

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 여행사]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You are planning a vacation with a friend. Call a travel agency, explain the vacation you and your friend would like, and ask three or four questions about how to plan the trip.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there's a problem that I need you to resolve. You bought a non-refundable plane ticket, but you can't travel because something came up. Call the travel agent, explain the situation, and suggest some alternative solutions.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Have you ever had trouble making vacation plans? Talk about what happened and how you solved the problem.

------------------------------------------------------------
[가전제품 (돌발)]
Q14. Many homes nowadays have new appliances or electronic devices that make life more convenient. Tell me about some new home appliances or electronic devices. How have they changed households?

Q15. Tell me about a modern household appliance or device that people find useful or convenient. What do people think about this home appliance or device?
