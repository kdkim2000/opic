Opic25 — 문제 스크립트 (여우 MOCK TEST 25, 난이도 5~6)

[Background Survey]
1. 일 경험 없음
2. 학생 - 아니오, 수업 등록 후 5년
3. 독신자로서 개인 주택이나 아파트에 거주
4. (총 8개) 영화보기, 콘서트 가기, 공연/연극 관람하기, 공원 가기, 해변가기, 카페/커피전문점 가기
5. (총 1개) 음악감상
6. (총 3개) 조깅, 걷기, 신체 운동을 즐기지 않음
7. (총 2개) 국내여행, 해외여행

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[산업 / 회사 / 커리어 (돌발)]
Q2. Can you tell me about some companies that young people want to work for? Why do they want to work at these companies?

Q3. Tell me about a rising company or industry in your country. Why is it growing? How has this company or industry achieved success?

Q4. What have you done for your career? What efforts have you made to get your job or improve your work skills?

------------------------------------------------------------
[음악 감상]
Q5. You indicated in the survey that you like to listen to music. What kind of music do you listen to? Who is your favorite musician or composer?

Q6. How did you first become interested in listening to music? What kinds of music did you like listening to in the past, and how has your taste in music changed over time?

Q7. Tell me about a time you listened to live music. Was it at a concert? Who did you go with? Was it memorable?

------------------------------------------------------------
[호텔 (돌발)]
Q8. Tell me about the hotels in your country. What are they like? Where are they usually located?

Q9. Can you tell me about a memorable hotel experience? What was the hotel called, and where was it? What was the place like? Why do you remember that hotel?

Q10. When was the last time you stayed in a hotel? Where was it? Why did you go there? Tell me in detail what you did at the hotel.

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 재활용]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You just moved into a new apartment and you are wondering about recycling. Call the management office and ask three or four questions about how to recycle.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there's a problem I need you to resolve. Your new neighbor from abroad put garbage in the recycling bin, and tenants are complaining about it. Talk to your neighbor and explain how to recycle properly.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Tell me how people recycled when you were a child. Was there a place for recycling? How was recycling in the past different from how we recycle now?

------------------------------------------------------------
[국내여행]
Q14. You indicated in the survey that you take vacations domestically. A lot of people say that traveling has become more difficult in the past five years. Can you tell me what kinds of changes you have noticed in traveling? How have these changes affected travelers?

Q15. What are the main issues or concerns when people talk about traveling? What are the causes of these concerns? How do people deal with them?
