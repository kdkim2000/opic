Opic30 — 문제 스크립트 (여우 MOCK TEST 30, 난이도 5~6)

[Background Survey]
1. 일 경험 없음
2. 학생 - 아니오, 수업 등록 후 5년
3. 독신자로서 개인 주택이나 아파트에 거주
4. (총 8개) 영화보기, 콘서트 가기, 공연/연극 관람하기, 공원 가기, 해변가기, 카페/커피전문점 가기
5. (총 1개) 음악감상
6. (총 3개) 조깅, 걷기, 신체 운동을 즐기지 않음
7. (총 2개) 국내여행, 해외여행

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[음악 감상]
Q2. You indicated in the survey that you listen to music. What kind of music do you listen to, and who are some of your favorite musicians or composers?

Q3. When and where do you usually listen to music? Do you listen to the radio, or do you go to concerts?

Q4. When did you first become interested in music? What kind of music did you first listen to, and how has your taste in music changed over time?

------------------------------------------------------------
[공원 가기]
Q5. You indicated in the survey that you go to parks. Tell me about a park you like to visit. What is it like? What do people usually do there?

Q6. What do you usually do at the park? What activities do you enjoy? Describe a typical day at the park from beginning to end.

Q7. Can you tell me about a memorable experience you had at a park? Was it interesting? Tell me what happened in detail.

------------------------------------------------------------
[휴일 / 명절 (돌발)]
Q8. Tell me about your country's holidays. Where do people usually celebrate those holidays? How do they celebrate them?

Q9. Tell me about a holiday you celebrated as a child. Where did you go, what did you do, and what are your memories of that holiday?

Q10. What did you do on your last holiday? Who did you spend it with, and what activities did you do? Was it memorable for you?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 기술 산업 / 친구]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You are writing a report on the technology industry. Your friend is an expert in this field. Call your friend and ask three or four questions to learn more about the technology industry.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there's a problem I need you to resolve. You have an appointment to meet your friend to learn more about the industry they work in. However, a few days before the meeting, a family member has an emergency. Call your friend, explain the situation, and suggest two or three alternatives to get the information.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Talk about an exciting new product you have heard of. For example, it could be a phone, car, video game, or some other product. How did you learn about it? Tell me about the product in detail.

------------------------------------------------------------
[음식 / 식품 (돌발)]
Q14. How has the way people buy food changed over the years? Where did people usually buy food in the past? Where do they usually buy it now?

Q15. Food can get contaminated by bacteria. Food can go bad or become toxic. Tell me about something you heard on the news about this issue.
