Opic26 — 문제 스크립트 (여우 MOCK TEST 26, 난이도 5~6)

[Background Survey]
1. 일 경험 없음
2. 학생 - 아니오, 수업 등록 후 5년
3. 독신자로서 개인 주택이나 아파트에 거주
4. (총 8개) 영화보기, 콘서트 가기, 공연/연극 관람하기, 공원 가기, 해변가기, 카페/커피전문점 가기
5. (총 1개) 음악감상
6. (총 3개) 조깅, 걷기, 신체 운동을 즐기지 않음
7. (총 2개) 국내여행, 해외여행

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[해변 가기]
Q2. Tell me about your favorite beach. What does it look like? Where is it, and how often do you go there?

Q3. When you go to the beach, what do you usually do, and who do you usually go with?

Q4. When was the last time you went to the beach? What did the beach look like? What did you do? Who did you go with?

------------------------------------------------------------
[집 / 거주]
Q5. I'd like to talk about where you live. Tell me about your home. What does it look like? How many rooms does it have?

Q6. Can you tell me about some problems you have had in your home? For example, something broke or someone you lived with didn't cooperate with you.

Q7. Pick one of the problems you mentioned above and describe it in detail. What was the problem? What caused it, and how did you handle it?

------------------------------------------------------------
[포장 / 배달 음식 (돌발)]
Q8. Tell me about your favorite takeout or delivery restaurant. What kind of food do they offer? What is the place like, and why do you like it?

Q9. Tell me about the last time you ordered food from a takeout or delivery restaurant. What did you order, and how was the experience?

Q10. Tell me about a special time when you got food from a takeout or delivery restaurant, such as for a party. What was the occasion? How did you get the food? Was it a memorable experience?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 건강식품 가게]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. A new health food store recently opened near your home. Call the store and ask three or four questions to learn more about what they offer.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there's a problem I need you to resolve. You bought something at the store, but you just found a problem with it. Call the store, tell them about the issue, and discuss a solution.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Can you tell me about a memorable meal you had with your friends or colleagues? What did you eat? Who were you with? Where did you eat? What made it memorable?

------------------------------------------------------------
[국가 간 관계 (돌발)]
Q14. Tell me about a change in the relationship between your country and another country. It could be related to politics, the economy, sports, or culture.

Q15. Can you talk about a historic event that changed the relationship between your country and a neighboring country? For example, a treaty between the two countries, a cultural event, or the visit of a leader from another country.
