Opic17 — 문제 스크립트
※ 녹취를 바탕으로 정리. 반복 질문은 1회만 수록.
※ (*) 표시 = 녹취가 불완전하여 문맥에 맞춰 보완한 부분

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[인터넷 (돌발)]
Q2. What do people typically do on the Internet? Do they watch videos or play games? Tell me about the things people do online.

Q3. What do you usually do on the Internet? Do you like shopping, reading the news, or using social media? Tell me what you like to do online.

Q4. When did you first start using the Internet, and what did you do on it? Tell me in detail about your first experience using the Internet.

------------------------------------------------------------
[해변 가기]
Q5. Tell me about your favorite beach. What does it look like? Where is it, and how often do you go there?

Q6. When you go to the beach, what do you usually do? And who do you usually go with?

Q7. When was the last time you went to the beach? What did the beach look like? What did you do, and who did you go with?

------------------------------------------------------------
[집 / 거주]
Q8. Let's talk about where you live. Tell me about your home. What does it look like? How many rooms does it have? Also, tell me about your favorite room.

Q9. Tell me about the home you lived in as a child. How was it different from the home you live in now?

Q10. Tell me about a change you made to your home. Why did you make that change, and how do you feel about it?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 해외여행 / 날씨]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You are going on vacation to another country. Call the hotel where you will be staying and ask about the weather there because you want to know what clothes to bring. Ask two or three more questions about your travel plans.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there is a problem I need you to resolve. You have arrived in the country, but the clothes you brought don't match the weather. Call a clothing store and explain your situation. (*) Ask two or three questions to solve the problem.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Have you ever had trouble because of unexpected weather? What happened, who were you with, and how did you solve the problem?

------------------------------------------------------------
[해외여행]
Q14. How has traveling abroad changed over the years? Is it easier or more difficult now compared to the past?

Q15. What are some important things people consider when traveling overseas? What are they typically interested in seeing or doing while traveling?
