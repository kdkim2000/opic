Opic18 — 문제 스크립트 (여우 MOCK TEST 18, 난이도 5~6)

[Background Survey]
1. 일 경험 없음
2. 학생 - 아니오, 수업 등록 후 5년
3. 독신자로서 개인 주택이나 아파트에 거주
4. (총 8개) 영화보기, 콘서트 가기, 공연/연극 관람하기, 공원 가기, 해변가기, 카페/커피전문점 가기
5. (총 1개) 음악감상
6. (총 3개) 조깅, 걷기, 신체 운동을 즐기지 않음
7. (총 2개) 국내여행, 해외여행

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me a little about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[재활용 (돌발)]
Q2. Let's talk about recycling. How do people recycle? What types of things do they recycle?

Q3. How do you recycle in your daily life? What types of things do you typically recycle?

Q4. I'd like to know how people recycled when you were a child. How did they recycle, and what did they recycle? Where did they put recyclables?

------------------------------------------------------------
[국내여행]
Q5. In the survey, you indicated that you travel domestically. Tell me about a city or place you enjoy visiting. What do you like about it?

Q6. What do you usually prepare before a trip? Please list everything you prepare.

Q7. Tell me about a trip you took when you were a child. Where did you go, who did you go with, and what did you do?

------------------------------------------------------------
[휴대폰 (돌발)]
Q8. What do you like about your phone? Is there a specific feature you enjoy, such as the camera or a particular app? Also, explain why you like that feature.

Q9. Tell me about your first phone. How is it different from your current one?

Q10. Have you ever had any problems with your phone, such as poor connectivity or battery issues? Tell me about a time when you had trouble using it.

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 공원]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. Your friend has invited you to the park this weekend. Call your friend and ask three or four questions about going to the park.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there's a problem I need you to resolve. You were supposed to go to a park with your friend this weekend, but you just found out that the park will be closed for renovations. Call your friend to explain the situation and suggest alternative plans.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Have you ever had a memorable experience at a park? What happened? Was it a good or bad experience? Why was it so memorable?

------------------------------------------------------------
[음악 감상]
Q14. In the background survey, you mentioned that you like to listen to music. Choose two different kinds of music or two composers and compare them in detail. What are their similarities and differences?

Q15. Can you tell me about some electronic gadgets or equipment related to music that people are interested in these days? What makes them special, and why are they so popular?
