Opic23 — 문제 스크립트 (여우 MOCK TEST 23, 난이도 5~6)

[Background Survey]
1. 일 경험 없음
2. 학생 - 아니오, 수업 등록 후 5년
3. 독신자로서 개인 주택이나 아파트에 거주
4. (총 8개) 영화보기, 콘서트 가기, 공연/연극 관람하기, 공원 가기, 해변가기, 카페/커피전문점 가기
5. (총 1개) 음악감상
6. (총 3개) 조깅, 걷기, 신체 운동을 즐기지 않음
7. (총 2개) 국내여행, 해외여행

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[영화 보기]
Q2. You mentioned in the survey that you go to the movies. What kinds of movies do you like to watch? Why do you enjoy those kinds of movies?

Q3. Tell me about a memorable movie you have watched. What was it about? What made it special?

Q4. Can you tell me about an actor or actress you recently saw in the news? What was the news about? Did you find it interesting?

------------------------------------------------------------
[식당 / 외식 (돌발)]
Q5. Tell me about the restaurants in your country. What are they like?

Q6. What do you usually do when you go to a restaurant? What steps do you take when you eat out?

Q7. Can you tell me about a time you ate at a restaurant when you were a child? Where did you go? What was the restaurant like? Who did you go with? Was the experience special to you?

------------------------------------------------------------
[기술 (돌발)]
Q8. What kinds of technology are commonly used in your country? Tell me about some of the technology people use, such as phones and computers.

Q9. You may have noticed that technology has changed over the years. What was technology like when you were a child? How has it changed since then?

Q10. Have you ever had problems dealing with technology? Maybe one of your devices stopped working or broke. Tell me about one of your experiences. How did you solve the problem?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 렌터카]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You are visiting New York on vacation, and you want to rent a car from an agency. Ask the agent three or four questions about renting a car for a week.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there's a problem that I need you to resolve. The agency isn't sure they can rent you a car because you have a non-US driver's license. Explain what kind of license you have and why it is valid for renting a car.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Tell me about an interesting experience that you had while you were on vacation. Why was it memorable? Was it a positive or negative experience? Tell me when and where it happened, and who you were with.

------------------------------------------------------------
[휴일 / 명절 (돌발)]
Q14. Tell me about some holidays in your country. What do people do on those holidays? How do they celebrate them?

Q15. Talk about some issues or concerns people have about holidays. Why do people have concerns about them, and how do they deal with them?
