Opic19 — 문제 스크립트 (여우 MOCK TEST 19, 난이도 5~6)

[Background Survey]
1. 일 경험 없음
2. 학생 - 아니오, 수업 등록 후 5년
3. 독신자로서 개인 주택이나 아파트에 거주
4. (총 8개) 영화보기, 콘서트 가기, 공연/연극 관람하기, 공원 가기, 해변가기, 카페/커피전문점 가기
5. (총 1개) 음악감상
6. (총 3개) 조깅, 걷기, 신체 운동을 즐기지 않음
7. (총 2개) 국내여행, 해외여행

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[음악 감상]
Q2. You indicated in the survey that you listen to music. What kind of music do you listen to, and who is your favorite musician or composer?

Q3. When and where do you usually listen to music? Do you listen to the radio, or do you go to concerts?

Q4. How did you first become interested in music? What kinds of music did you listen to in the past, and how has your taste in music changed over the years?

------------------------------------------------------------
[교통수단 (돌발)]
Q5. Describe transportation in your country. How do people usually get around, and what modes of transportation are available?

Q6. How do you get around? What types of transportation do you use? Do you drive, or do you use public transportation?

Q7. What means of transportation did you use when you were a child? How did people get around in your city back then?

------------------------------------------------------------
[해외여행]
Q8. You indicated in the survey that you take vacations internationally. Tell me about a city or country you've visited. What was it like, and what were the people like?

Q9. Tell me about your first trip abroad—when and where did you go, who did you travel with, and what did you do?

Q10. Have you had an unforgettable experience while traveling? What happened, and what made it so memorable?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 자동차 고장]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You are driving through a small town and have car trouble. Go to a service station for help. Ask three or four questions to get the information you need to get your car fixed.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there's a problem I need you to resolve. You will miss an important business meeting because of your car trouble. Call your client. Explain the situation and offer some possible solutions.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Tell me about a time when you had trouble with your car. What was the problem, and how did you solve it?

------------------------------------------------------------
[카페 / 커피전문점]
Q14. How have the reasons for visiting coffee shops changed over the years? What do people usually do at coffee shops? Why do people go to coffee shops other than just drinking coffee?

Q15. Coffee shops and coffee houses are popular all around the world. Why have they become so popular? What role do they play in people's daily lives?
