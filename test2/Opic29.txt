Opic29 — 문제 스크립트 (여우 MOCK TEST 29, 난이도 5~6)

[Background Survey]
1. 일 경험 없음
2. 학생 - 아니오, 수업 등록 후 5년
3. 독신자로서 개인 주택이나 아파트에 거주
4. (총 8개) 영화보기, 콘서트 가기, 공연/연극 관람하기, 공원 가기, 해변가기, 카페/커피전문점 가기
5. (총 1개) 음악감상
6. (총 3개) 조깅, 걷기, 신체 운동을 즐기지 않음
7. (총 2개) 국내여행, 해외여행

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[집 / 거주]
Q2. I'd like to talk about where you live. Tell me about your favorite room in your home. What does it look like? Why do you like that room?

Q3. Tell me about some housework you do at home. What do you do to keep your house nice and clean?

Q4. Tell me about the home you lived in as a child. How was it different from the home you live in now?

------------------------------------------------------------
[지형 / 자연 (돌발)]
Q5. Tell me about the geography of your country. Are there many mountains, rivers, or lakes?

Q6. Tell me about a special place you visited as a child. Maybe it had a unique geographical landmark or feature. What did you see, and what was your impression of that place?

Q7. Can you tell me about your experience visiting a natural place in your country? What activities did you do there? Did you go hiking or enjoy the beach?

------------------------------------------------------------
[건강 (돌발)]
Q8. Can you tell me about a healthy person you know? What is he or she like? Why do you think that person is healthy?

Q9. Have you ever done something to stay healthy, such as eating healthy food or working out? What did you do, and how did you feel after trying it?

Q10. You might have tried to improve your health, like quitting smoking or drinking. Perhaps you signed up for an exercise program. What did you try, and what was your reason for doing it?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 휴대폰 구매]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You would like to buy a new mobile phone. Call a store and tell them what you need, then ask three or four questions to find out about the phones they offer.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there's a problem that I need you to resolve. When you bought the new phone, you realized that it did not have all the features you need. You would like to return it to get a new phone. Call the store, explain the situation, and make arrangements to exchange it for one that has the features you need.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Have you ever bought a piece of technology which was not what you wanted or different from what you had expected? Was the feature not what you wanted or did it just not work properly? Tell me exactly what was wrong and what you did to solve the situation.

------------------------------------------------------------
[포장 / 배달 음식 (돌발)]
Q14. A lot of fast-food, take-out, or delivery restaurants are offering healthy choices these days. Why do you think these kinds of restaurants have made such changes?

Q15. Tell me about the latest trends related to take-out or delivery food options. What recent changes have you noticed regarding takeout or delivery food?
