## Q2. 한국의 재활용

**Q:** Tell me about recycling in your country. How do people recycle, and what do they recycle?

> **Recycling is a big deal in Korea.** **We take it very seriously.** **Almost everyone sorts their trash** into **paper, plastic, glass, cans, and food waste**. **Each apartment complex has a recycling area**, and **there are separate bins for each type**. **We have to rinse the containers** and **take off the labels**, **otherwise it's a pain for the workers**. **Food waste is a separate system, too.** **In many places, you have to pay for a special trash bag** for **general waste**, **so people try to throw away less**. **It was a hassle at first**, **but now it's second nature**. **I think it's a good habit**, **especially for a country with limited space**.

- 핵심: `a big deal`, `sort the trash`, `second nature`, `a pain`, `limited space`

## Q3. 나의 재활용 방법 (단계별)

**Q:** How do you recycle? Do you recycle every day? Where do you take the recyclable items? Tell me all the steps you take for recycling.

> **I don't recycle every day**, but **we collect things all week**. **Here's how it works.** **First, we separate everything** in the kitchen: **plastic bottles, cans, paper, and so on**. **Second, we rinse the containers**, **and I'm very strict about this**. **Third, we remove the labels** and **flatten the boxes** to **save space**. **Then, we put everything into separate bags.** **Once a week, usually on Wednesday evening**, **I carry the bags down to the recycling area** in our apartment complex. **I sort them into the right bins**, **and food waste goes into a special machine** with **a card**. **It's a short trip**, **but it's become part of my weekly routine.** **And I usually grab a coffee on my way back!**

- 핵심: `Here's how it works`, `First, ~ Second, ~ Third, ~`, `flatten`, `part of my routine`
- 순서 표현(First / Second / Third / Then)이 IH 포인트입니다.

>
> **I ran back downstairs.** **It was already dark**, and **the bins were full**. **So there I was, using my phone as a flashlight**, **digging through a pile of cardboard boxes.** **A neighbor walked by and gave me a very strange look.** **I just smiled and said, "I'm looking for something."** **Finally, after about 20 minutes, I found it!** **I came back covered in dust**, and **my friend couldn't stop laughing** when I told the story. **Since then, I always check the box twice** before I throw it away.

## Q5. 한국의 명절과 기념일

**Q:** Let's talk about holidays in your country. What holidays are there, and how are they celebrated?

> **We have quite a few holidays in Korea.** **The two biggest ones are Seollal, which is Lunar New Year, and Chuseok.** **On Seollal, we wear traditional clothes**, **called hanbok**, and **bow to our elders** to **wish them a happy new year**. **We eat rice cake soup**, **and kids get pocket money.** **Chuseok is more like Thanksgiving.** **We visit our hometowns** and **share a big meal** with family. Besides those, **there's Children's Day** in May, **Buddha's Birthday**, **and Hangul Day**, which **celebrates our alphabet**. **And of course, Christmas** is **a holiday, too**, **but it's more of a couples' day**. **On most holidays, people take time off**, **spend time with family**, **and eat a lot of food**.

- 핵심: `quite a few`, `bow to our elders`, `more like ~`, `take time off`

## Q6. 어린 시절의 명절 (설날, 가족과)

**Q:** Tell me about a holiday experience you had when you were young. What did you do, and who did you spend the holiday with?

> **When I was a kid, my favorite holiday was Seollal.** **I spent it with my family** at **a house with a small yard**. **The whole house was packed**, **and it was chaos in the best way.** **In the morning, we all put on our hanbok**, and **I had to bow to my elders**. **I hated it at first**, **but then they gave me pocket money!** **I counted it every year.** **We ate rice cake soup**, **and the other kids and I played yut**, **a traditional board game**, **on the floor.** **Of course, the other kids always cheated**, **and I always cried**. **But we always ended up laughing.** **Looking back, those were the best days.** **I miss that noisy house**.

- 핵심: `packed`, `in the best way`, `bow to ~`, `cheat`, `end up laughing`
- 윷놀이(yut)는 한국 명절 소재라 흥미를 끕니다.

## Q15. 집을 구할 때 겪는 문제와 해결

**Q:** Tell me about issues people have when they rent a house or an apartment. What are the things people are concerned about when renting a house or an apartment, and how do they deal with them?

> **Renting a place can be stressful**, **and people worry about a lot of things.** **The biggest concern in Korea is the deposit.** **We often pay a huge deposit**, **and people are afraid of losing it** if **the landlord can't pay it back.** **There are also scams**, **so people have to be careful.** **Other common concerns are the rent being too high**, **the condition of the place**, and **noisy neighbors.**
>
> **How do people deal with these problems?** **They usually check the building's records online** to **make sure the landlord really owns it.** **They also go through a real estate agent**, **visit the place in person**, **and read the contract carefully.** **Some people buy deposit insurance.** **In my case, being in IT, I'd check everything twice.** **My advice is: don't rush, and always ask questions.**

- 핵심: `deposit`, `landlord`, `scams`, `make sure ~`, `read the contract carefully`
