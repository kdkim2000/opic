And **in the office, it's pretty common to wear business casual**. **Overall, I'd say Koreans care a lot about how they look.**

## Q14. 해외여행의 변화

**Q:** You indicated in the survey that you take vacations internationally. How has traveling abroad changed over the years? Is it easier or more difficult to travel compared to the past?

> **I think traveling abroad is a lot easier now.** **When I took my first trip abroad, I had to buy paper tickets**, **carry a thick guidebook**, and **exchange cash at the airport.** **These days, I can do almost everything on my phone.** **I book flights and hotels online**, **use a translation app**, and **pay with my card.** **Plus, there are more cheap flights**, **so more people can afford to travel.** **Last winter, my friends and I went to Da Nang, in Vietnam**, **and we planned the entire trip on our phones.**
>
> **On the other hand, there are some downsides.** **Popular places get really crowded.** **Prices have gone up**, **and airports are busy.** **Also, because everyone posts photos on social media**, **it's harder to find hidden gems.** **But overall, I'd say it's easier**, **and it's a lot more fun.**

- 핵심: `a lot easier`, `I can do almost everything on my phone`, `afford to ~`, `hidden gems`, `On the other hand`

**Q:** Tell me about the things that are important to people when they travel overseas. What are the things people are interested in seeing? What are the things that are important for travelers to do?

> **As for what travelers should do, I'd say they should respect the local culture.** **Learn a few basic phrases**, **like "hello" and "thank you".** **It goes a long way.** **It's also a good idea to plan ahead.** **One of my friends is great at this.** **For our Da Nang trip, she made a detailed schedule**, **and everything went smoothly.** **And finally, stay open-minded.** **Things won't always go as planned**, **but that's part of the adventure.**

- 핵심: `travel insurance`, `local specialties`, `landmarks`, `It goes a long way`, `plan ahead`, `go smoothly`
