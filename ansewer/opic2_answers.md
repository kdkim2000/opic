# OPIc 모의고사 2 — 내 답변 스크립트 (최종본 v2)

목표는 IH (레벨 5-5)입니다. 쉬운 표현과 미국인이 실제 쓰는 구어체로, 유창하게 이어 말하는 것이 기준입니다. 자기소개(Q1)는 모든 세트 동일합니다.

- 답변 길이는 약 60~80초(8~12문장)입니다.
- 다 외우지 말고 **굵은 표현**(뼈대)만 외우세요.
- `...` 표시는 롤플레이에서 상대방이 대답하는 구간입니다. 1~2초 쉬세요.
- ⚠️ = 아직 확인하지 않은 임시값입니다.

## Q1. 자기소개

**Q:** Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

> Hi, how's it going? I'm **a big coffee lover**, so I guess I'll start there. **I can't really start my day without a cup of coffee**, and this morning was no different. I had one before I came to take this test, and I'm **already looking forward to** another one once it's over. I work **in IT**, and my days are pretty busy. I live in an apartment, and I'm **pretty much a homebody**. In my free time, I like listening to music, playing computer games, and reading. So, yeah, that's me **in a nutshell**: **coffee, music, and my computer**. I'm happy to be here, and I hope I do well today!

## Q2. 좋아하는 음악과 가수

**Q:** What kind of music do you like listening to? Who is your favorite singer or composer?

> **I'm into a lot of different music**, but if I had to pick, I'd say **K-pop**. When I'm working, though, **I like to put on some jazz or lo-fi music** in the background. It **helps me focus**, and it goes really well with **a cup of coffee**. As for my favorite group, it's **a girl group** that I discovered a few years ago. **I've been a fan of theirs for a while now.** Their songs are **catchy** and **full of energy**, and **their vocals are really strong**. And here's the fun part: **one of the members is from a small city near mine**, so **I feel a special connection** to them, and **I'm kind of proud of her**. **Whenever I need to unwind after a long day, I put on their playlist**, and **it just melts the stress away**.

- 핵심: `I'm into ~`, `helps me focus`, `catchy`, `a special connection`, `melt the stress away`
- 좋아하는 그룹 이름을 말하고 싶으면 `it's called ~`만 덧붙이세요.

## Q3. 음악을 듣는 때와 장소

**Q:** When and where do you usually listen to music? Do you listen to music on the radio, or do you go to concerts?

> **I listen to music pretty much all the time.** **On my way to work**, I listen in the car. **At my desk**, I put on headphones and play music while I work. And at home, **I play music on my computer** when I'm gaming or reading in my room. I mostly **stream music on my phone**, so **I don't really listen to the radio** anymore. As for concerts, **I've actually never been to one**. **Honestly, I'd love to go**, especially **to a concert by my favorite group**. **I'm sure the energy is totally different** from listening at home. **Maybe I'll finally go next year**, **and I might even drag a friend along**!

- 핵심: `pretty much all the time`, `stream music`, `I've never been to ~`, `drag a friend along`

## Q4. 음악 취향의 변화

**Q:** When did you first become interested in music? How has your interest in music changed over time? How is the music you liked as a child different from the music you like now?

> **I got into music in middle school.** I used to **carry around a portable CD player**, and I'd **listen to the same album over and over again**. Back then, I liked **loud, upbeat songs**, like rock and dance music. **I wanted music that was fun and full of energy.** These days, though, **my taste has changed a bit**. I still like K-pop, but I also **go for calmer songs**, like jazz and lo-fi, **especially when I'm working**. I think it's because **my life is so busy now** that I want something **relaxing**. Also, **the way I listen has changed**. In the past, I had to **buy CDs**. Now I can **stream anything I want** in seconds. **Looking back, it's kind of funny** how much things have changed.

- 핵심: `got into ~`, `over and over again`, `go for ~`, `taste has changed`, `stream`

## Q5. 자주 가는 해변

**Q:** Tell me about a beach you like to go to. Where is it? What does it look like? How often do you go there?

> **My favorite beach is a small one near my house**, which is **right here in my city**. **I live in a small city by the sea**, so **we have lots of beaches**, but **this one is my favorite** because **it's close to my home**. **It only takes about 20 minutes by car.** **It's not a huge beach**, but **it's quiet and peaceful**. The water is **clear and calm**, and **you can see boats and islands** in the distance. **The sunsets are beautiful**, too. There are also **a few cute cafes** nearby, which is a big plus for me. **I go there a couple of times a month** in the summer, **usually with a friend**. **It's my go-to place** when I want to **clear my head**. **I'm lucky to live so close to the sea.**

- 핵심: `right here in ~`, `quiet and peaceful`, `in the distance`, `my go-to place`, `clear my head`
- 차로 20분으로 확정했습니다.

## Q6. 최근에 다녀온 해변 (올여름 서해안)

**Q:** Tell me about the beach that you recently went to. What did it look like? What did you like about the beach?

> **The last time I went to the beach was this past summer.** **A friend and I took a road trip along the west coast**, and **we stopped at a beach** on the way. **It was very different from the beaches near my city.** **It was incredibly wide and flat**, and **the sand was soft and warm**. **The water was far out**, **so we took off our shoes** and **walked along the shore**. **What I liked most was the sunset.** **We sat on the sand** with **iced coffee**, of course, and **just watched the sun go down**. **The whole sky turned red.** I **took a ton of pictures**, but **none of them really did it justice**. **It was so relaxing**, and **I felt like all my stress had disappeared**.

- 핵심: `along the shore`, `very different from ~`, `did it justice`, `a ton of ~`

## Q7. 기억에 남는 해변 여행 (갯벌 이야기)

**Q:** Tell me about a memorable trip to the beach. Why did you go? What did you do there? What made it so memorable?

> **One trip I'll never forget was the west coast road trip** I took with a friend **this summer**. **We wanted to get away** after **a really stressful month at work**. **My city is on the south coast**, and **the tide there doesn't change much**. So **when we got to the west coast, the tide was out**, and **the sea had gone out so far** that **there was a huge mudflat** instead of water. **I'd never seen anything like it!** **We decided to walk out onto the mudflat**, **barefoot**, and **we were collecting little clams** and **chasing crabs**. We were **having so much fun** that **we lost track of time**.
>
> **Suddenly, I noticed the water was coming back in**, **really fast**. We **had to run** back to the shore, **laughing and screaming**. **I tripped, fell face-first into the mud, and got covered from head to toe!** My friend **couldn't stop laughing**. In the end, **we had to wash off at a gas station**. **It was messy, but it was one of the funniest days of our lives.** **That's why it was so memorable.**

- 핵심: `the tide was out`, `mudflat`, `lost track of time`, `from head to toe`, `couldn't stop laughing`
- 남해안 도시는 조수 차이가 작다는 점을 넣어서 "갯벌이 처음이라 신기했다"는 이유가 자연스럽습니다.
- 지어낸 부분은 "진흙에 넘어짐"입니다. 시험에서는 사실 여부가 중요하지 않습니다.

## Q8. 한국의 모임과 축하 행사

**Q:** Tell me about gatherings and celebrations in your country. How do people celebrate them, and what do they do when they get together?

> **We have lots of gatherings and celebrations** in Korea. **The biggest ones are Chuseok and Lunar New Year.** On those holidays, **the whole family gets together**, usually at **the grandparents' house**. We **prepare a big meal**, **eat a lot of traditional food**, and **catch up with each other**. **Kids get pocket money** from their elders, which they love. Besides that, **we celebrate birthdays**, **weddings**, and **a baby's first birthday**. And **in the workplace, we have company dinners** with coworkers, which we call "hoesik". In general, **food is at the center of everything**. **People eat, drink, talk, and laugh** together for hours. **That's how we show we care about each other.**

- 핵심: `get together`, `catch up with ~`, `at the center of ~`, `show we care`

## Q9. 마지막으로 다녀온 모임 (추석, 부모님 집)

**Q:** Tell me about the last gathering or celebration you went to. What was it, and what did you do there?

> **The last one I went to was Chuseok**, **just a couple of weeks ago**. **I went to my parents' house**, since **it's close to mine**. **The whole family was there**, including **a lot of aunts, uncles, and cousins**. **We arrived in the morning**, and **everyone was already busy** in the kitchen. **I helped out** by **carrying the dishes** and **setting the table**. **We had a huge meal**: **rice cakes, grilled fish, and lots of side dishes**. After lunch, **we sat around and chatted**, and **I played cards with a few of my relatives**. Later in the afternoon, **we went on a short walk** to **burn off** some of the food. **It was just a simple day**, but **I really enjoyed it**. **Spending time with family always recharges me.**

- 핵심: `help out`, `set the table`, `sit around and chat`, `burn off`
- 온 가족과 친척이 모였다는 내용을 반영했습니다.

## Q10. 기억에 남는 모임 (친구 생일 깜짝 파티)

**Q:** Tell me about a memorable experience you had at a gathering or celebration. What happened, and why was it unforgettable?

> **One I'll never forget was a friend's birthday party** **last year**. **A few of us planned a surprise party**, and **I was in charge of keeping him away from the house**. **I took him out for a "quick coffee"**. **We ended up talking for two hours!** Meanwhile, **my friends kept texting me**: "**Keep him there a little longer!**" **I was sweating the whole time**.
>
> **When we finally got to the house, everyone was hiding in the dark**. **The lights came on**, and **everyone shouted "Surprise!"** **But my friend just stood there**, **completely confused**. **He thought he'd walked into the wrong house!** **Then he started laughing**, and **his eyes got a little teary**. **It was so touching.** **It was unforgettable** because **he's usually such a serious, quiet man**, **and that was the first time I saw him so emotional**.

- 핵심: `in charge of -ing`, `keep him away`, `We ended up -ing`, `teary`, `touching`
- 작년 생일로 정했습니다.

## Q11. 롤플레이: 가구 구매 문의

**Q:** You want to buy a piece of furniture for your house. Ask the salesperson at the furniture store three or four questions about the furniture you want to buy.

> Hi, **I'm looking for a desk** for my home office. **I saw a wooden one** on your website, **the "Oslo" model**, and **I'd like to ask a few questions**. **First, what are the dimensions?** **I need it to fit** in a small room. ... **Great. And what is it made of?** **Is it solid wood?** ... **I see. How many colors does it come in?** **I'm thinking about walnut** or **white**. ... **One last question: does the price include delivery and assembly?** **And how long does it take to arrive?** ... **That sounds good.** **Thank you so much for your help!**

- 핵심: `I'm looking for ~`, `What are the dimensions?`, `Is it made of ~?`, `Does the price include ~?`
- 질문은 4개입니다. (크기 / 소재 / 색상 / 배송·조립)

## Q12. 롤플레이: 가구 문제 해결

> Hello, **I'm calling about a desk I bought** from your store **last week**. **I have a bit of a problem.** **When I put it together, I found a big scratch** on the top, and **one of the legs doesn't fit properly**, so **the desk wobbles**. **I'm really disappointed**, because **I was looking forward to using it**. **First, could you send someone to fix it** at my house? **If that's not possible**, **could you send me a brand-new desk**? **Or, if you don't have any in stock, I'd be happy to get a refund**. **Any of those would work for me.** **I'd really appreciate your help**. Thank you!

## Q13. 구매 후 문제 경험 (에스프레소 머신)

**Q:** Have you ever had a problem with something you bought? What was the problem, and how did you resolve it?

> Yes, and **it involved my biggest passion: coffee**. **A few years ago**, I **splurged on an expensive espresso machine**. **I was so excited**. **The first morning, I made a cup**, and **I felt like a real barista**. **But the next day, water started leaking** all over the kitchen counter! **I thought maybe I'd done something wrong**, so **I read the manual** and **tried again**. **Same thing**. **It was clearly a defect.**
>
> **I called customer service**, and **they were kind of slow at first**. **But I sent them a video** of the leak, and **they agreed to send a replacement**. **A few days later, the new machine arrived**, and **it worked perfectly**. **Since then, I always check reviews** and **keep the receipt**. **And of course, I never go a day without my espresso!**

- 핵심: `splurge on ~`, `defect`, `replacement`, `keep the receipt`, `never go a day without ~`

## Q14. 두 사람 비교 (친구와 직장 동료)

**Q:** Choose two people among your family or friends and compare them. How are they similar, and how are they different?

> **I'd like to compare my best friend and one of my coworkers.** **They're actually quite similar** in some ways. **They're both really kind**, and **they both love trying new food**. **They also have a good sense of humor**, so **I laugh a lot** around both of them.
>
> **But they're very different in other ways.** **My friend is outgoing and spontaneous.** **She'll say, "Let's just go!"** and **figure it out later**. She **makes friends wherever she goes**. **My coworker, on the other hand, is shy and quiet**, and **she's a planner**. **When we go on a trip together, she makes a detailed schedule** for everything. In that way, **she's more like me**. **I think I need both of them in my life**. **One keeps me adventurous**, and **the other keeps me organized**.

- 핵심: `a good sense of humor`, `on the other hand`, `spontaneous`, `outgoing`, `shy`, `a planner`
- 친구는 외향적·즉흥적, 직장 동료는 내성적·계획적으로 정했습니다.

## Q15. 가족·친구와 나누는 대화, 마지막 대화

**Q:** What do you usually talk about with your friends or family members? Tell me about the last conversation you had with one of them.

> **With my family, we talk about pretty much everything**: **work, dinner plans, and where to go on the weekend**. **With my friends, it's mostly games, work stress, and old memories.** **We laugh about stupid things** from **the old days**. **The last conversation I had was with a friend**, **last night, actually**. **We were sitting on the couch** with **a cup of coffee**, and **we talked about where to go to see the fall foliage**. **My friend wanted to go to a famous mountain**, **but I said it'd be too crowded**. **We went back and forth for a while**, and **in the end, we decided to leave early** to **beat the crowds**. **It wasn't a serious talk**, but **I love moments like that**. **Those little conversations are what keep us close.**

- 핵심: `pretty much everything`, `go back and forth`, `beat the crowds`, `keep us close`
