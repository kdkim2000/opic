## Q1. 자기소개 (opic1~3과 동일)

**Q:** Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

> Hi, my name is Bread. I'm **a big coffee lover**, so I guess I'll start there. **I can't really start my day without a cup of coffee**, and this morning was no different. I had one before I came to take this test, and I'm **already looking forward to** another one once it's over. I work **in IT at Samsung Heavy Industries**. I'm **in charge of** our IT systems, so my days are pretty busy. I live in an apartment with my wife, and we're **pretty much homebodies**. In my free time, I like listening to music, playing computer games, and reading. So, yeah, that's me **in a nutshell**: **coffee, my wife, and my computer**. I'm happy to be here, and I hope I do well today!

## Q2. 떠오르는 산업 (친환경 조선)

**Q:** Tell me about a rising industry or company in your country. What is it, and why is that industry or company rising and becoming popular?

> **One industry that's really on the rise is shipbuilding**, especially **eco-friendly ships**. **I live in Geoje**, which is **a shipbuilding city**, so **I see it with my own eyes**. **Korea has been building ships for decades**, and **we're known for high-quality, high-tech vessels**. **Lately, there's been a big push for cleaner energy**, so **shipping companies around the world want ships that produce less pollution**, like **LNG carriers**. **Korean shipyards are among the best at building them.** On top of that, **the industry is going digital**. **We're using AI and smart systems** to **build ships faster and safer**. **That's why orders have been pouring in.** **It's a great time to work in this industry**, and **I'm proud to be a small part of it**.

- 핵심: `on the rise`, `with my own eyes`, `a big push for ~`, `orders have been pouring in`, `a small part of it`

## Q3. 유망한 회사 (내가 다니는 회사)

**Q:** Tell me about a promising company in the industry you mentioned above. Why is the company promising? What benefits does it bring to your society?

> **The company I'd like to talk about is the one I work for**, **Samsung Heavy Industries**. **Honestly, I might be a little biased**, **but I really think it's promising.** **First, it has strong technology**, especially in **LNG carriers** and **smart shipyard systems**. **Second, it keeps investing in the future**, like **autonomous ships** and **cleaner fuels**. **As for the benefits to society, there are a lot.** **It creates thousands of jobs** here in Geoje, **not just for the employees, but also for local restaurants, shops, and small suppliers.** **The whole city depends on it.** **It also brings in a lot of money from exports**, which **helps the national economy**. **When the shipyard does well, Geoje does well.** **That's why I feel proud every time I go to work.**

- 핵심: `a little biased`, `keeps investing in ~`, `creates thousands of jobs`, `When X does well, Y does well`

## Q4. 회사의 최근 변화

**Q:** Tell me about recent changes in the company you mentioned above. For example, the company released a new product. What changes have there been, and how have they affected the company?

> **There have been a lot of changes recently.** **The biggest one is the digital transformation.** **We've introduced AI and smart systems** to **our shipyard**, so **we can track the progress of a ship in real time**. **And since I'm in IT, I'm right in the middle of it!** **For example, we started using sensors and cameras** to **spot safety problems early**. **That has made the workplace safer.** **We've also started building more eco-friendly ships**, **which are in high demand right now**. **As a result, the company has received more orders**, and **our work has been getting busier**. **Honestly, it's been a lot of pressure for my team.** **But it's exciting**, because **we're doing something new.** **I think these changes will make the company stronger** **in the long run**.

- 핵심: `digital transformation`, `in real time`, `in high demand`, `As a result`, `in the long run`

> **I like K-pop concerts**, **especially girl group concerts**, like **Rescene**. **I'll be honest: I haven't been to many**, **but I love the idea of them.** **I like them for a few reasons.** **First, the energy is unreal.** **The lights, the dancing, and the crowd singing along**: **you just can't get that from a recording.** **Second, it's a great way to forget about work.** **For two hours, you're not thinking about servers or emails.** **And third, it makes me feel young again!** **I also like small outdoor concerts**, **where you can just relax on the grass**. **It's less crowded**, **and the atmosphere is friendly.** **If I could, I'd go to one every month.**

## Q8. 가장 좋아하는 국내 여행지 (통영)

**Q:** You indicated in the survey that you enjoy traveling domestically. What is your favorite location, and why do you like that place?

> **My favorite place in Korea is Tongyeong.** **It's right next to Geoje**, **so it's easy to get to**, **but it feels like a different world.** **It's a port city with beautiful islands**, and **it's sometimes called "the Naples of Korea."** **I love it for three reasons.** **First, the scenery is stunning**: **you can ride a cable car up the mountain** and **look down at dozens of little islands.** **Second, the food is amazing**, **especially the fresh seafood**. **I always get the raw fish** and **a bowl of seafood stew.** **And third, there are lots of cute cafes** with **ocean views**, **which is perfect for a coffee lover like me.** **Whenever I need a mini getaway, I go to Tongyeong.** **It always makes me feel refreshed.**

- 핵심: `a different world`, `stunning`, `a mini getaway`, `feel refreshed`

## Q9. 젊었을 때의 여행 (대학 시절 강릉 기차여행)

**Q:** Tell me about a trip you took in your youth. Where did you go, and who did you go with? Why did you go there?

> **When I was in college, I went on a trip to Gangneung** with **three of my best friends.** **We were broke students**, so **we took the cheapest train we could find.** **We went there to see the sunrise at the beach**, **because we'd heard it was a famous spot.** **We stayed in a tiny, cheap room**, **and all four of us slept on the floor.** **We woke up at 5 a.m.**, **freezing**, **and walked to the beach in the dark.** **When the sun finally came up over the ocean, nobody said a word.** **It was so beautiful.** **Then we ate hot noodles** at **a small stand**, **and it was the best thing I'd ever tasted.** **We didn't have much money**, **but we had so much fun.** **Those were the days!**

- 핵심: `broke`, `freezing`, `nobody said a word`, `Those were the days!`

## Q10. 국내여행 중 기억에 남는 경험 (제주 한라산 우박)

**Q:** Have you had any interesting or unforgettable experiences while you were traveling in your own country? Tell me what happened in detail.

> **Yes, one trip to Jeju Island stands out.** **My wife and I decided to hike Hallasan**, **the tallest mountain in Korea.** **The weather was perfect when we started.** **But about halfway up, the sky suddenly turned dark**, **and it started to hail!** **The hailstones were as big as peanuts**, **and they hurt!** **We had no umbrellas.** **We hid under a tree**, **but we were still getting hit.** **My wife started laughing and said**, "**This is what we get for not checking the forecast!**"
>
> **After about ten minutes, it stopped**, **and the sun came out.** **And just then, a huge rainbow appeared** over the mountain. **It was breathtaking.** **We were soaking wet and our legs were sore**, **but we were so happy.** **We finally reached a beautiful viewpoint**, **and we shared a hot cup of instant coffee** from my backpack. **It was the best coffee ever.** **That's why I never skip the weather forecast now**!

- 핵심: `stands out`, `halfway up`, `hail`, `breathtaking`, `soaking wet`
- 거제의 폭우 정전 이야기와 겹치지 않도록 날씨 소재를 제주 우박으로 바꿨습니다.

## Q13. 약속을 취소한 경험 (태풍으로 취소)

**Q:** Tell me about an experience when you had to cancel an appointment you made. What happened? Why did you have to cancel the appointment, and how did you deal with the situation?

> **I remember a time when I had to cancel** **an important appointment.** **It was a dental checkup**, **which I'd waited three weeks to get.** **But that morning, a typhoon was coming** to **the south coast.** **The winds were so strong** that **the bridge connecting Geoje to the mainland was closed.** **And since the clinic was on the mainland**, **there was no way to get there.** **I was so annoyed.** **I called the clinic**, **explained the situation**, and **apologized**. **They were very understanding**, and **they said they get a lot of calls like that** during **typhoon season.** **We rescheduled for the following week.**
>
> **In the end, I spent the day at home**, **with a cup of coffee** **watching the rain.** **My wife said**, "**Maybe it's the universe telling you to relax.**" **She was right.** **Since then, I always check the weather** **before I book anything.**

- 핵심: `a typhoon was coming`, `no way to get there`, `understanding`, `the following week`
- 거제와 육지를 잇는 다리 설정으로 지역성을 살렸고, 앞선 폭우 에피소드와 달리 "일정 취소" 중심입니다.

## Q14. 지형이 비슷한 나라 (일본)

**Q:** Tell me about a country that has similar geographical features to your country. What changes has that country had in recent years?

> **A country that's similar to Korea is Japan.** **Both countries are surrounded by water**, and **both have lots of mountains.** **There's not much flat land in either country**, so **people live close together in the cities.** **Japan also has thousands of islands**, **just like Korea.** **There are differences**, **of course**: **Japan is bigger**, **and it has more volcanoes and earthquakes.**
>
> **In recent years, Japan has changed a lot.** **The biggest change is probably tourism.** **The yen has become weaker**, **so Japan has become a very cheap place to visit.** **A lot of foreign tourists have been going there**, **including many Koreans.** **Also, the population is getting older**, **which is a big issue.** **Korea is facing the same problem.** **I visited Osaka years ago**, **and I'd love to go back** **to see how it's changed.**

- 핵심: `surrounded by water`, `not much flat land`, `weaker yen`, `facing the same problem`
