## Q2. 한국의 교통수단

**Q:** Tell me about transportation in your country. What types of transportation do people use?

> **Korea has an excellent transportation system.** **In big cities like Seoul, most people take the subway or the bus.** **The subway is fast, clean, and cheap**, and **you can get almost anywhere** with it. **For long distances, there's the KTX**, **our high-speed train**. **It can take you from Seoul to Busan in about two and a half hours.** **Taxis are also everywhere**, and **you can call one with an app**. **Here in Geoje, it's a little different.** **We're on an island, so most people drive**, **and we use bridges and tunnels** to **get to the mainland**. **One thing I love is the T-money card**: **you tap it once, and it works on buses, subways, and even taxis.** **Honestly, getting around Korea is really convenient.**

- 핵심: `an excellent transportation system`, `get almost anywhere`, `high-speed train`, `tap`, `convenient`

## Q3. 평소 이용하는 교통수단

**Q:** What kind of transportation do you use to get around? Do you use public transportation, or do you drive?

> **I mostly drive.** **I live in Geoje**, and **public transportation isn't as convenient** here as in a big city. **I drive to work every day**, and **it takes about 25 minutes.** **I like driving**, because **I can listen to my music** and **sip my coffee** on the way. **I basically turn my car into my own little cafe!** **When I go to Seoul or other big cities, though, I take the KTX**, since **driving there is a nightmare** with **all the traffic and parking**. **I also take the bus sometimes**, **especially when my wife and I want to have a few drinks.** **Overall, I prefer the freedom of having my own car.** **I can go wherever I want, whenever I want.**

- 핵심: `a nightmare`, `turn my car into ~`, `sip`, `the freedom of ~`

## Q4. 대중교통에서 겪은 문제 (졸다가 정류장을 놓침)

**Q:** Sometimes riding a subway or bus can be uncomfortable. Have you ever had any problems while taking public transportation?

> **Yes, I have.** **The most common problem is crowding.** **During rush hour, the bus is packed like a can of sardines.** **You can hardly move**, and **sometimes you get stuck in the corner**. **But the most embarrassing experience was when I fell asleep on a bus.** **It was after a company dinner**, **and I'd had a few drinks.** **I was so tired that I dozed off.** **When I woke up, the bus was parked at the last stop**, **and the driver was staring at me!** **I had no idea where I was.** **It was almost midnight**, and **there were no more buses.**
>
> **I had to take a taxi all the way home**, and **it cost me a fortune.** **My wife just shook her head.** **Since then, I always set an alarm on my phone** when **I take the bus late at night**. **Lesson learned!**

- 핵심: `packed like sardines`, `doze off`, `cost me a fortune`, `Lesson learned`

## Q5. 자주 가는 캠핑장

**Q:** Where do you enjoy going camping? Describe the place where you often go camping in as much detail as you can.

> **My favorite place to go camping is a campsite in Namhae**, **which is a short drive from Geoje.** **It's next to a small valley**, **so you can hear the water running all night.** **The campsite is surrounded by tall pine trees**, **and the air is so fresh.** **It has a flat, grassy area for tents**, **a clean bathroom**, and **a small shop** where **you can buy firewood and ice cream.** **At night, the sky is full of stars.** **You can see them so clearly because there's no city light.** **There's also a short walking trail** that **leads up to a lookout point**, **with a view of the sea.** **I like it because it's quiet**, **and it's the perfect spot to unplug from work.** **I always make a pot of coffee in the morning**, **and it tastes ten times better outdoors.**

- 핵심: `a short drive from ~`, `surrounded by ~`, `unplug from work`, `ten times better`

## Q6. 캠핑을 처음 한 때와 계기

**Q:** When did you first go camping? How did you become interested in camping?

> **I went camping for the first time about five years ago.** **I got interested in it by watching camping videos on YouTube.** **I'm always on my computer**, **so I'd watch people sitting by a campfire**, **cooking food, and drinking coffee** in **the middle of the woods.** **I thought, "That looks so relaxing."** **One day, I told my wife**, "**Let's try it!**" **She wasn't so sure at first**, **because she's not an outdoor person.** **We borrowed a tent from a friend**, and **we went to a campsite for one night.** **It was a bit of a disaster**: **we couldn't set up the tent**, **and the mosquitoes were terrible.** **But we loved the campfire**, **and we were hooked.** **After that, we bought our own gear.**

- 핵심: `got interested in ~`, `a bit of a disaster`, `we were hooked`, `gear`

## Q7. 기억에 남는 캠핑 (텐트 폴을 두고 감)

**Q:** Tell me about your most memorable camping trip. Where did you go? What did you do during your camping trip? Why was it so memorable?

> **My most memorable camping trip was to the campsite in Namhae**, **with my wife, my daughter Eunbin, and my son Suhyun.** **We drove there on a Friday evening**, **and it was already getting dark** when **we arrived.** **When I opened the trunk to set up the tent, I realized something terrible**: **I'd forgotten the tent poles!** **I had left them at home, in the garage.** **My family just stared at me.** **I felt so embarrassed**, **especially because I'm the one who's always planning everything.**
>
> **But we didn't give up.** **I used the car's headlights** to **look around**, **and I found some long branches.** **Eunbin**, **who's very careful and smart**, **came up with a plan**: **we tied the tent to the car and to two trees.** **It looked ridiculous**, **but it worked!** **We grilled meat**, **roasted marshmallows**, and **looked at the stars.** **It was the best camping trip we've ever had.** **We still laugh about it**, **and now I have a checklist** on my phone.

- 핵심: `I'd forgotten ~`, `stared at me`, `came up with a plan`, `It looked ridiculous, but it worked`
- 에피소드: opic1의 폭우 정전이나 남원 고장과 겹치지 않는 "장비를 두고 온" 이야기입니다.

## Q13. 여행 준비 중의 문제 (여권 만료)

**Q:** Have you ever had a problem while planning for a trip? What was the problem, and how did you solve the problem?

> **Yes, I have, and it was a real scare.** **A few years ago, my wife and I planned a trip abroad.** **We had booked the flights and the hotel**, **and we were so excited.** **About two weeks before the trip, I was checking my documents**, **and I noticed something terrible: my passport was about to expire.** **Many countries require at least six months of validity**, **and mine had only three months left!**
>
> **I panicked.** **The next day, I took a day off work**, **and went to the city hall** to **apply for a new passport.** **Usually, it takes about a week**, **but I explained my situation**, **and they helped me out.** **I got the new passport just two days before the flight.** **Phew!** **Since then, I always check the expiration date** on **my passport first** when **I plan a trip.** **Even an IT guy can forget the basics!**

- 핵심: `a real scare`, `about to expire`, `validity`, `helped me out`, `Phew`

## Q14. 영화: 어릴 때 vs 지금

**Q:** Compare the movies today to the movies you watched when you were a child. How have movies changed over the years? Tell me the similarities and differences between them.

> **Movies have changed a lot.** **When I was a kid, I watched movies on a small TV**, **usually on video tapes** that **my sisters rented.** **We'd all squeeze together on the floor**, **eating snacks.** **Going to the movie theater was a special treat**, **because it only happened once or twice a year.** **Now, I can watch almost any movie at home**, **on a big screen, with Netflix or other streaming apps.** **The special effects are also much better.** **They look so real**, **and there's even 4D and IMAX.**
>
> **But some things haven't changed.** **A good story is still the heart of a movie.** **And I still love eating popcorn** while **watching one**. **The biggest difference, I think, is the "experience."** **We used to wait for a movie to come out.** **Now we can watch it anytime**, **which is convenient**, **but a little less special.**

- 핵심: `squeeze together`, `a special treat`, `special effects`, `the heart of ~`, `a little less special`
