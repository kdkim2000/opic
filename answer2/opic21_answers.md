## Q2. 방문한 나라 (방콕)

**Q:** You indicated in the survey that you take vacations internationally. Tell me about a city or country you've visited. What was it like, and what were the people like?

> **One place I'd love to go back to is Bangkok, in Thailand.** **We went with some friends a few years ago.** **To be honest, I didn't expect much**, **but the city blew me away.** **It was loud, hot, and crowded**, **but it was full of energy.** **There were golden temples next to tall skyscrapers**, **and tuk-tuks zipping around everywhere.** **And the street food!** **I ate so much pad thai that I lost count.** **But what really got me was the people.** **They call Thailand "the land of smiles"**, **and it's true.** **Everyone was so kind and relaxed**, **even when I made a mistake.** **When I tried to say "thank you" in Thai**, **the vendor smiled so big, I felt like a local.** **It was a feel-good trip from start to finish.**

## Q4. 첫 해외여행 (오사카, 입국심사)

**Q:** Tell me about your first trip abroad—when and where did you go, who did you travel with, and what did you do?

> **My first trip abroad was to Osaka, in Japan**, **with a good friend.** **It was a long time ago**, **back in my college days.** **We were so excited**, **but I was also nervous about one thing: immigration.** **I'd heard stories** about **long lines** and **tough questions.** **So I practiced my answers in my head** the whole flight. **When it was finally my turn**, **the officer looked at my passport**, **looked at me**, **and said in English**, "**Purpose of visit?**" **My mind went blank!** **I said, "Um... eating?"** **My friend burst out laughing** because **I sounded so silly.** **The officer smiled and let us through.**
>
> **After that, the trip was a breeze.** **We visited the castle**, **ate a ton of food**, and **rode the trains.** **To this day, I still get teased** about **that moment**, **but I just blame the jet lag!**

- 핵심: `immigration`, `my mind went blank`, `burst out laughing`, `a breeze`, `to this day`
