## Q2. 한국의 은행

**Q:** Tell me about the banks in your country. What are they like? Where are they typically located?

> **Well, banks in Korea are everywhere.** **You can find one on almost every busy street**, **especially downtown** **and near big office buildings.** **They're usually in tall buildings**, **on the first or second floor**, **and often a few banks are right next to each other.** **Inside, they're clean and quiet.** **You take a number**, **and wait for your turn on a comfy sofa.** **And the staff are super polite.** **They bow and say, "How can I help you?"** **Also, there are ATMs** **at almost every convenience store**, **so you can get cash at midnight.** **And these days**, **we have mobile-only banks too**, **like KakaoBank.** **To sum up**, **banks here are convenient**, **friendly, and easy to find.**

- 핵심: `on almost every busy street`, `take a number`, `super polite`, `To sum up`

## Q4. 은행의 변화 (통장에서 앱으로)

**Q:** How have banks in your country changed over time? What were banks like when you were a child, and how are they different now?

> **Banks have changed a lot.** **When I was a kid, everything was on paper.** **I remember my dad taking me to the bank to open my first account.** **They gave me a little passbook**, **and the teller stamped it** **every time I put in money.** **I felt so important!** **Every time I saved my allowance**, **I'd go to check the number.** **And when I saw the interest**, **I felt like I was getting rich!** **Also, banks closed at 4 p.m.**, **so you had to run there** **after school or work.** **The lines were really long**, **too.** **Now, it's totally different.** **I can do everything on my phone**, **24 hours a day.** **I don't even need a card.** **It's so convenient**, **but part of me misses that little passbook.**

- 핵심: `passbook`, `felt so important`, `totally different`, `part of me misses ~`
