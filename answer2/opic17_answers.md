# OPIc 모의고사 17 — 내 답변 스크립트 (최종본 v1)

목표는 IH (레벨 5-5)입니다. 쉬운 표현과 미국인이 실제 쓰는 구어체로, 유창하게 이어 말하는 것이 기준입니다. 자기소개(Q1)는 모든 세트 동일합니다.

- 답변 길이는 약 60~80초(8~12문장)입니다.
- 다 외우지 말고 **굵은 표현**(뼈대)만 외우세요.
- `...` 표시는 롤플레이에서 상대방이 대답하는 구간입니다. 1~2초 쉬세요.
- 임의 스토리 항목은 실제 경험이 있으면 바꿔서 쓰세요.

## Q1. 자기소개

**Q:** Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

> Hi, how's it going? I'm **a big coffee lover**, so I guess I'll start there. **I can't really start my day without a cup of coffee**, and this morning was no different. I had one before I came to take this test, and I'm **already looking forward to** another one once it's over. I work **in IT**, and my days are pretty busy. I live in an apartment, and I'm **pretty much a homebody**. In my free time, I like listening to music, playing computer games, and reading. So, yeah, that's me **in a nutshell**: **coffee, music, and my computer**. I'm happy to be here, and I hope I do well today!

## Q2. 사람들이 인터넷에서 하는 일

**Q:** What do people typically do on the Internet? Do they watch videos or play games? Tell me about the things people do online.

> **Well, I'd say people do just about everything on the Internet these days.** **The number one thing is watching videos.** **People watch YouTube** on **their commute**, **during lunch**, and **even in bed.** **Then there's social media.** **People post photos of their food** and **check what their friends are up to.** **A lot of folks also shop online.** **You can order anything at night**, **and it shows up at your door the next morning.** **Of course, people play games, too**, **and some even make a living from it.** **And don't forget about the practical stuff**, like **banking, booking trips, and reading the news.** **Honestly, I think the Internet has become like air.** **If it's gone, everyone panics.** **I see that all the time** when **our network goes down at work!**

- 핵심: `just about everything`, `check what ~ are up to`, `show up at your door`, `make a living from ~`, `like air`

## Q3. 내가 인터넷에서 하는 일

**Q:** What do you usually do on the Internet? Do you like shopping, reading the news, or using social media? Tell me what you like to do online.

> **To be honest, I spend a ridiculous amount of time online.** **Since I work in IT, I'm on the Internet all day.** **But for fun, there are three things I do.** **First, I read tech news over my morning coffee.** **That's my little routine.** **Second, I watch YouTube videos about games and new gadgets.** **I can lose a whole evening that way.** **And third, I shop for coffee beans online.** **I'm kind of obsessed with it.** **Last month, I ordered beans from a tiny roaster in Jeju**, **and they were fantastic.** **I'm not really into social media.** **I check it once in a while**, **but I mostly just look at other people's photos.** **My friends say I'm the only person who doesn't post anything.** **I tell them, "I'm a lurker, not a poster!"**

- 핵심: `a ridiculous amount of time`, `lose a whole evening`, `be kind of obsessed with ~`, `be not really into ~`

## Q4. 처음 인터넷을 쓴 경험 (PC방, 전화선 모뎀)

**Q:** When did you first start using the Internet, and what did you do on it? Tell me in detail about your first experience using the Internet.

> **I first used the Internet in high school**, **back in the late 90s.** **We didn't have a computer at home**, **so my friends and I went to a PC room.** **I still remember it like it was yesterday.** **The computer was so slow.** **A single photo took about a minute to load**, **line by line!** **I didn't even know what to search for.** **A friend told me to try a chat room**, **so I did.** **I typed "hello" to a total stranger**, **and a girl from Busan wrote back.** **My heart was racing.** **We chatted for two hours**, **and it turned out she was a fifty-year-old man.** **I'm kidding!** **I think she was just a nice college student.** **Anyway, I was hooked.** **Right after that, I began playing online games.** **That first night is the reason I work in IT today.**

- 핵심: `back in the late 90s`, `like it was yesterday`, `turned out to be ~`, `I was hooked`
- 지어낸 이야기입니다. 농담 부분은 빼도 됩니다.

## Q5. 가장 좋아하는 해변 (몽돌해변)

**Q:** Tell me about your favorite beach. What does it look like? Where is it, and how often do you go there?

> **My favorite beach is a pebble beach near my city.** **It's about 30 minutes from my apartment by car.** **It's not a sandy beach**, **which is what makes it special.** **The shore is covered with smooth, round black pebbles.** **When the waves roll in and out, the stones make this soft rolling sound.** **It's like natural music.** **Behind the beach, there's a forest of camellia trees**, **and in front of you there's the clear, blue ocean.** **It's not too crowded**, **except for a few weeks in the summer.** **I'd say I go about once a month.** **Whenever I feel stressed, I go there with a cup of coffee**, **and I just sit and listen to the waves.** **It's like therapy to me.**

- 핵심: `what makes it special`, `smooth, round pebbles`, `roll in and out`, `It's like therapy`

## Q6. 해변에서 하는 일과 같이 가는 사람

**Q:** When you go to the beach, what do you usually do? And who do you usually go with?

> **I usually go to the beach with a friend.** **Sometimes a few other friends come along**, **but they're busy**, **so it's mostly the two of us.** **When we get there, the first thing we do is take a slow walk along the shore.** **We don't talk much.** **We just enjoy the sound of the waves.** **Then we find a nice spot** and **sit down with takeout coffee.** **I'm not much of a swimmer**, **so I never go in the water**, **but my friend loves to dip his feet in.** **He picks up little stones**, **and he says every one is different.** **If we're hungry, we grab some grilled fish or noodles from a stand nearby.** **Honestly, we don't do anything special.** **But it's the best way to clear my head.**

- 핵심: `come along`, `take a slow walk`, `I'm not much of a ~`, `clear my head`

## Q7. 마지막으로 해변에 간 날 (지난달 가을)

**Q:** When was the last time you went to the beach? What did the beach look like? What did you do, and who did you go with?

> **The last time I went to the beach was about a month ago**, **on a Sunday in early fall.** **I went to a pebble beach near my house with two friends.** **It was a cloudy day**, **so the sky was gray**, **and the sea looked a little moody.** **But the beach was practically empty**, **which was perfect.** **The summer crowds were gone**, **and it was so quiet that we could hear every wave.** **We walked from one end to the other.** **One of my friends and I had a contest** to **see who could skip a stone more times.** **He beat me**, **of course.** **And he never lets me forget it!** **Afterward, we sat on the pebbles and shared a thermos of hot coffee.** **My other friend wrapped herself in a blanket.** **It was cold, but it felt so good.** **We went home feeling refreshed.**

- 핵심: `practically empty`, `skip a stone`, `never lets me forget it`, `feeling refreshed`

## Q8. 우리 집과 가장 좋아하는 방

**Q:** Let's talk about where you live. Tell me about your home. What does it look like? How many rooms does it have? Also, tell me about your favorite room.

> **Well, I live in an apartment.** **It's a pretty normal Korean apartment.** **We have three bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen, and two bathrooms.** **It's not huge**, **but it's cozy**, **and the light is great in the morning.** **The living room is where I hang out**, **and it has a big, comfy sofa.** **But my favorite room is, without a doubt, my own room.** **It's small**, **but it's got everything I need**: **my computer, a bed, and a bookshelf full of novels.** **At night, I put on my headphones**, **play a game or read**, **and I forget about the rest of the world.** **It's my little hideout.** **Even my friends say**, **"That's his hideout, don't knock!"**

- 핵심: `hang out`, `without a doubt`, `my little hideout`, `forget about the rest of the world`

## Q9. 어린 시절 집과 지금 집 비교 (감나무와 강아지)

**Q:** Tell me about the home you lived in as a child. How was it different from the home you live in now?

> **I grew up in an old house with a small yard.** **It had a persimmon tree right in the middle**, **and every fall, we picked the fruit.** **I remember our dog, Happy**, **running around that tree** **like crazy.** **As a kid, I was always in someone's way.** **The house was loud**, **and the bathroom was always taken.** **I had to wait in line behind everyone!** **In winter, the bathroom was freezing**, **so I held it in as long as I could.** **My apartment now is a totally different story.** **It's warm**, **quiet**, **and I have my own room.** **The only thing I miss is the yard.** **I'd love to sit under that persimmon tree with a cup of coffee.** **But I wouldn't trade my warm bathroom for it.**

- 핵심: `be in someone's way`, `wait in line`, `a totally different story`, `I wouldn't trade ~ for it`

## Q10. 집에 한 변화 (내 방 정리)

**Q:** Tell me about a change you made to your home. Why did you make that change, and how do you feel about it?

> **Last year, I finally redid my room.** **For years, it was a total mess.** **Cables everywhere**, **a tiny desk**, **and a monitor that was too small.** **My friend kept saying**, "**You're going to ruin your eyes!**" **So one weekend, I decided to just do it.** **I bought a bigger desk**, **a nice big monitor**, and **a proper chair.** **I also put up sound panels on the wall**, **so I don't bother the neighbors with my game sounds.** **It took me two full days**, **and I put the desk together the wrong way twice.** **My friend just laughed.** **But when I sat down for the first time, I was so happy.** **My back doesn't hurt anymore**, **and everything feels organized.** **I only wish I had done it sooner.** **It's the best money I've ever spent.**

- 핵심: `redo my room`, `a total mess`, `put together`, `I wish I had done it sooner`
- 지어낸 이야기입니다. 실제로 바꾼 것이 있으면 그걸로 바꾸세요.

## Q11. 롤플레이: 호텔에 날씨와 여행 계획 문의

**Q:** I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You are going on vacation to another country. Call the hotel where you will be staying and ask about the weather there because you want to know what clothes to bring. Ask two or three more questions about your travel plans.

> Hello, **I have a reservation at your hotel next month.** **I'm going to be staying with my family for five nights.** **I'm calling because I'm not sure what to pack.** **How's the weather in Sapporo in March?** ... **Oh, really? So I should bring a heavy coat?** ... **Got it. Is it still snowing then, or is it rainy?** **Should I bring waterproof boots?** ... **Thank you. Also, I have a few more questions.** **Is there a shuttle bus from the airport to the hotel?** ... **And what time is check-in?** **Our flight lands in the morning**, **so can we leave our bags with you?** ... **Last thing: does the hotel have a laundry service?** ... **Perfect. Thanks so much for your help!**

- 핵심: `I'm not sure what to pack`, `How's the weather in ~?`, `Is there a shuttle bus?`, `leave our bags with you`
- 질문은 5개입니다. (날씨 / 눈·비 / 셔틀 / 체크인 / 세탁)

## Q12. 롤플레이: 날씨에 안 맞는 옷, 옷 가게에 전화

**Q:** I'm sorry, but there is a problem I need you to resolve. You have arrived in the country, but the clothes you brought don't match the weather. Call a clothing store and explain your situation. Ask two or three questions to solve the problem.

> Hello, **I'm a tourist, and I have a bit of a problem.** **I just arrived in Sapporo**, **and it's much colder than I expected.** **The hotel said it'd be mild**, **so I only packed light jackets.** **Now I'm freezing**, **and my family is, too.** **I was wondering if you could help me.** **First, do you sell warm coats and gloves?** ... **Great. Do you have sizes for adults and a teenager?** ... **How much would a coat cost?** **I'd like something that isn't too expensive.** ... **Also, what time do you close today?** **We're at a hotel near the station**, **so is it okay if we come right now?** ... **And could you deliver to the hotel**, **just in case?** ... **Thank you so much. You're a lifesaver!**

- 핵심: `I have a bit of a problem`, `I was wondering if ~`, `just in case`, `You're a lifesaver`
- 구조: 상황 설명 → 필요한 물건 → 사이즈·가격 → 영업시간·배달

## Q13. 예상치 못한 날씨로 겪은 어려움 (지어낸 이야기: 제주 태풍)

**Q:** That's the end of the situation. Have you ever had trouble because of unexpected weather? What happened, who were you with, and how did you solve the problem?

> **Yes, I have.** **A few years ago, my friends and I went to Jeju for a long weekend.** **The forecast looked fine**, **so we didn't worry about it.** **But on the second day, a typhoon came out of nowhere.** **The wind was so strong that we could barely walk.** **All the flights were canceled.** **We were stuck on the island**, **and we were all disappointed.** **I was so stressed, because I had to be back at work on Monday.**
> **So I stayed calm and made a plan.** **First, I called the airline and rebooked the next flight.** **Then I found a hotel near the airport.** **One of my friends made it fun**: **we ordered pizza** and **played cards in the room.** **We ended up having a great time.** **The next day, the weather cleared up**, **and we got home safe.** **The lesson I learned is to always have a plan B.** **And check the weather twice!**

- 핵심: `out of nowhere`, `be stuck on ~`, `rebook`, `plan B`, `end up -ing`
- 지어낸 이야기입니다.

## Q14. 해외여행의 변화

**Q:** How has traveling abroad changed over the years? Is it easier or more difficult now compared to the past?

> **Traveling abroad has become so much easier.** **Let me tell you about my first trip overseas.** **I had a paper map**, **a dictionary**, and **a printed ticket.** **I got lost within an hour**, **and I had to show my map to strangers.** **It was a total mess!** **Nowadays, things are completely different.** **I just open a map app**, **and it tells me exactly where to go.** **If I can't read a sign**, **I take a photo with my phone**, **and it translates it right away.** **I can also pay with my phone**, **so I don't even need cash.** **Plus, many countries don't require a visa for Koreans anymore.**
> **That said, there's one downside.** **Because everything is so easy, trips feel a bit less adventurous.** **I kind of miss getting lost.** **But I'd still choose the easy way.**

- 핵심: `so much easier`, `get lost`, `translate it right away`, `That said`

I go for the coffee shops!** **I always search for a good local cafe.**

- 핵심: `compare prices`, `I learned that the hard way`, `it depends on the person`, `go for ~`
