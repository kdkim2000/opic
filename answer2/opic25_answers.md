Another reason is **the tech companies feel cool and modern.** **You can wear hoodies to work**, **bring your own laptop**, and **work on stuff** millions of people use. **But the thing is, the competition is crazy.** **Some people study for years just to get one interview.** **I think that's why so many young people feel stressed out.** **Anyway, I get it. ## Q13. 어릴 때의 재활용과 지금 비교
