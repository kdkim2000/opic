## Q2. 한국의 건강식

**Q:** What kinds of healthy foods do people eat in your country? What are these foods, and where do people buy them?

> Well, **when it comes to healthy food, Koreans are really serious about it**. **The first thing that comes to mind is kimchi.** **It's packed with good bacteria**, and **we eat it with almost every meal.** **We also eat a lot of rice with vegetables**, like **bibimbap**, and **soups with tofu or seaweed.** **My friend says seaweed soup is the best thing for your body.** **And when it's hot outside, people eat samgyetang**, **which is chicken soup with ginseng.** **It sounds strange, but it really works!** **As for where people buy it, most folks go to a big supermarket or a traditional market.** **Honestly, I like the traditional market better**, **because the vegetables are fresh and cheap.** **These days, a lot of people order groceries online**, **and they show up at your door before dawn.** **That's pretty convenient.**

- 핵심: `when it comes to ~`, `be packed with ~`, `the first thing that comes to mind`, `show up at your door`

## Q3. 건강을 지키기 위해 하는 일 (건강검진 이후 변화)

**Q:** What do you do to stay healthy? Tell me about the things you do to maintain your health.

> **To be honest, I wasn't always careful about my health.** **I sit at a desk all day**, **drink way too much coffee**, and **I'm not a gym guy at all.** **But two years ago, my checkup results came back**, **and the doctor said my cholesterol was a little high.** **That was a wake-up call.** **So I made a few small changes.** **First, I take the stairs at work**, **even though my office is on the 7th floor!** **Second, my friend and I take a walk along a nearby beach after dinner**, **at least three times a week.** **And I cut back on late-night snacks**, **which was the hardest part.** **At my next checkup, the numbers were back to normal.** **I felt so proud of myself.** **I still drink my coffee, of course**, **but now I keep it to three cups a day.** **Well, maybe four.**

- 핵심: `a wake-up call`, `cut back on ~`, `I'm not a gym guy`, `back to normal`

## Q4. 어릴 때와 지금의 건강 관리 변화

**Q:** How has the way people maintain their health changed? What did people do to stay healthy when you were a child, and what do they do now?

> **Things have changed a lot.** **When I was a kid, people mostly stayed healthy by eating well at home.** **My mom made chicken soup whenever someone caught a cold.** **There was always somebody telling me to wear a jacket and wash my hands!** **And if you felt sick, you just drank hot water and went to bed early.** **Nobody went to a gym back then.** **You stayed in shape by playing outside all day.** **These days, it's a whole different story.** **People use apps and smartwatches** to **count their steps** and **track their sleep.** **Gyms and yoga studios are everywhere**, **and people take vitamins and protein shakes.** **I even wear a smartwatch myself**, **and it buzzes at me when I sit too long.** **So I'd say we used to rely on family habits, and now we rely on technology.**

- 핵심: `catch a cold`, `stay in shape`, `a whole different story`, `rely on ~`

## Q15. 집을 빌릴 때 흔한 문제들

**Q:** What are some common problems people face when renting a home? Why do these problems occur, and how do people solve them?

> **There are a few problems that come up a lot when people rent a home.** **The biggest one is probably the deposit.** **In Korea, you often pay a huge deposit when you rent**, **and some landlords don't give it back on time.** **That happens because the landlord is short on cash**, **or the next tenant hasn't shown up yet.** **Another common issue is repairs.** **Something breaks, like the heater or a faucet**, **and the landlord says, "That's not my problem."** **There's also noise from the neighbors**, **and sometimes mold on the walls in the summer.**
> **So how do people solve these problems?** **First, they read the contract very carefully before signing.** **Second, they take photos of everything** **when they move in**, **so no one can argue later.** **And if things go wrong, people talk to the landlord politely first**, **and if that doesn't work, they ask for help from a real estate agent or a legal office.** **I think the key is to put everything in writing.**

- 핵심: `come up a lot`, `be short on cash`, `That's not my problem`, `put everything in writing`
