## Q2. 한국 사람들의 재활용 방식

**Q:** Let's talk about recycling. How do people recycle? What types of things do they recycle?

> **Well, Koreans take recycling very seriously.** **We separate almost everything.** **Plastic goes in one bin**, **paper in another**, **cans and glass in another.** **Food waste has its own special bag**, and **everything else goes in a paid trash bag.** **If you throw your trash in the wrong place, your neighbors will notice!** **In my apartment complex, there's a special recycling area** **with big colored containers.** **Everyone brings their stuff there once a week.** **People recycle all kinds of things**: **plastic bottles, cardboard boxes, newspapers, and cans.** **Before you throw out a plastic bottle, you have to take the label off** and **rinse it out.** **It's a lot of work, honestly.** **But it's become a habit, and I think it's a good one.** **Korea is actually one of the best countries at recycling.**

- 핵심: `take ~ seriously`, `separate everything`, `paid trash bag`, `rinse it out`, `become a habit`

## Q3. 내가 일상에서 하는 재활용 (가족이 엄격함)

**Q:** How do you recycle in your daily life? What types of things do you typically recycle?

**Q:** I'd like to know how people recycled when you were a child. How did they recycle, and what did they recycle? Where did they put recyclables?

> **Back when I was a kid, recycling was very different.** **We didn't have those colored bins.** **Instead, we reused things as much as we could.** **For example, we took empty soda bottles back to the corner store**, **and the owner gave us a little money for each one.** **My friends and I used to collect them and trade them for candy!** **We also saved old newspapers and metal cans** **in the corner of the yard.** **Every few weeks, an old man with a truck came by**, **shouting, "Old newspapers! Scrap metal!"** **He'd weigh them**, **and sometimes he gave us a bar of soap or a roll of toilet paper.** **As for regular trash, we burned it in the yard.** **That's not allowed anymore**, **of course.** **It wasn't as organized as it is now**, **but I'd say we wasted less.**

- 핵심: `Back when I was a kid`, `as much as we could`, `trade ~ for ...`, `come by`
