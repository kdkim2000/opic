**Q:** You mentioned in the survey that you go to the movies. What kinds of movies do you like to watch? Why do you enjoy those kinds of movies?

> Well, **I'm kind of into sci-fi and thrillers.** **I love movies** that **make me think**, **or keep me on the edge of my seat.** **Since I work with computers all day**, **I guess I'm drawn to stories about technology and the future.** **And honestly, movies are my way of escaping reality.** **When the lights go off in the theater**, **I forget about work and just get lost in the story.** **My friend is different.** **She's a total romantic comedy fan.** **So we take turns**: **one week it's my pick**, **and the next week it's hers.** **I'll admit**, **I've cried at a few of her movies**, **but don't tell anyone!** **I think a good movie is the best way to unwind.**

**Q:** Tell me about a memorable movie you have watched. What was it about? What made it special?

> **A movie I'll never forget is "Interstellar".** **It's about a farmer who used to be a pilot.** **The Earth is dying**, **so he has to leave his kids behind and travel through space** **to save humanity.** **I watched it in the theater** **with a friend**, **and I was blown away by the visuals.** **But what really got me was the father-and-kids story.** **There's a scene where he watches video messages from his kids**, **and they've grown up while he was away.** **He just breaks down and cries.** **I couldn't help but tear up**, **because I was thinking about my family back home.** **I work long hours**, **so that scene hit me really hard.** **That's what made it special.** **It wasn't just a space movie**, **it was about love and time.**

## Q5. 한국의 식당

**Q:** Tell me about the restaurants in your country. What are they like?

> **Korea has restaurants everywhere**, **and there's something for everyone.** **The most popular ones are Korean BBQ places**, **where you grill the meat at your table.** **There are also chicken and beer joints**, **and little street tents** **called "pojangmacha."** **The thing I love about Korean restaurants is the service.** **First, you get a lot of free side dishes**, **and you can ask for refills for free!** **Also, almost every table has a call button.** **You just press it**, **and a server comes right away.** **My American friends were shocked when they saw it!** **And delivery is huge in Korea.** **You can get almost anything delivered in thirty minutes.** **Overall, I think Korean restaurants are fast, friendly, and a great value.**

- 핵심: `something for everyone`, `The thing I love about ~`, `call button`, `a great value`

## Q6. 외식할 때 하는 일 (단계)

**Q:** What do you usually do when you go to a restaurant? What steps do you take when you eat out?

> **Well, it all starts with picking a place**, **and that's always a battle!** **My friends and I never agree.** **So first, we check reviews on a map app.** **If it's a popular place, we put our name on the waiting list** **on an app**, **so we don't have to stand outside.** **When we get in, we sit down**, **and the first thing I do is order a cup of coffee** **or a drink.** **Then we look at the menu**, **and we order a few dishes to share.** **While we wait, we chat** **and take photos of the food.** **Once we're done, one of us pays** **with a phone**, **and we split the bill** **using a banking app.** **Finally, I always say**, **"Thank you, it was delicious!"** **It's a pretty normal routine**, **but I really enjoy every step.**

- 핵심: `it all starts with ~`, `put our name on the waiting list`, `share dishes`, `split the bill`

## Q7. 어린 시절 식당 (중국집 짜장면)

**Q:** Can you tell me about a time you ate at a restaurant when you were a child? Where did you go? What was the restaurant like? Who did you go with? Was the experience special to you?

> **Oh, I remember this clearly.** **When I was in elementary school**, **my family went to a Chinese restaurant** **on my graduation day.** **Eating out was a big deal back then**, **and going to a Chinese restaurant was the ultimate treat.** **My parents and a bunch of relatives came**, **so it was a big crowd!** **The restaurant was small**, **with red tables and a loud kitchen.** **My dad ordered jajangmyeon for everyone.** **When it came out, I was so excited**, **and I dove right in.** **But I was too fast**, **and the black sauce splashed all over my white shirt!** **My cousins teased me for weeks.** **But my mom just smiled**, **and she gave me her sweet and sour pork.** **It was a total mess**, **but it's one of my happiest childhood memories.**

**Q:** What kinds of technology are commonly used in your country? Tell me about some of the technology people use, such as phones and computers.

> **Korea is a real tech country.** **Everyone has a smartphone**, **even my grandma!** **People use their phones for everything**: **banking, shopping, ordering food, and even paying at a store.** **Honestly, I can't remember the last time I carried cash.** **We also have super fast internet**, **and free Wi-Fi almost everywhere**, **even on the subway.** **At work, of course, we use computers all day**, **and a lot of companies use cloud services** **and video meetings.** **Another thing is KakaoTalk.** **It's the messenger app that everybody uses**, **from kids to grandparents.** **If you don't have it, you're basically out of the loop.** **Overall, technology here is fast and convenient**, **and I think that's something Koreans are proud of.**

## Q9. 어린 시절 기술과 지금의 변화

**Q:** You may have noticed that technology has changed over the years. What was technology like when you were a child? How has it changed since then?

> **Wow, it's a completely different world now.** **When I was a kid, we had one phone in the house**, **and it was attached to the wall!** **If you wanted to call a friend**, **you had to call his house** **and ask his mom.** **Our TV had an antenna**, **and I had to go fix it when the picture got fuzzy.** **Then, in high school, we got our first computer.** **To use the internet, we had a dial-up modem.** **It made this weird screeching noise**, **and while I was online, nobody could use the phone.** **My family yelled at me all the time!** **Now, I carry a phone** **that's more powerful than that old computer.** **I can talk to anyone in the world** **with just a tap.** **It's crazy how fast things have changed**, **and I feel lucky to have seen both worlds.**

- 핵심: `a completely different world`, `dial-up modem`, `screeching noise`, `with just a tap`, `It's crazy how fast ~`

## Q10. 기술 문제 경험 (휴대폰이 물에 빠짐)

## Q13. 휴가 중 기억에 남는 경험 (괌, 바다거북)

**Q:** That's the end of the situation. Tell me about an interesting experience that you had while you were on vacation. Why was it memorable? Was it a positive or negative experience? Tell me when and where it happened, and who you were with.

> **One experience I'll never forget happened in Guam**, **about three years ago.** **I went with a few friends.** **It was a very positive experience.** **We rented a car**, and **one morning, we went snorkeling** at **a quiet beach.** **I'm not a great swimmer**, **so I was kind of nervous.** **But after a while**, **I got used to it.** **Then, out of the blue**, **a huge sea turtle swam right next to me!** **It was about as big as a dining table!** **I froze for a second**, **and then I just floated there.** **It looked at me**, **and then it glided away, very slowly.** **I screamed into my snorkel**, **and one of my friends heard me and swam over.**

## Q14. 한국의 명절 (설날과 추석)

**Q:** Tell me about some holidays in your country. What do people do on those holidays? How do they celebrate them?

> **Two of the biggest holidays in Korea are Seollal and Chuseok.** **Seollal is the Lunar New Year**, **and Chuseok is like Korean Thanksgiving.** **On both days, families get together.** **People travel back to their hometowns**, **so the roads are packed.** **In the morning, we wear hanbok**, **our traditional clothes.** **Then the kids bow to their elders**, **and they get money.** **That's the best part for them!** **On Seollal, we eat tteokguk**, **which is rice cake soup.** **On Chuseok, we make songpyeon**, **which are sweet rice cakes.** **At my place, a lot of relatives come over**, **so there are about twenty people in the house!** **It's loud and crazy**, **but it's the one time of year** **when everyone's together.** **It's tiring**, **but I wouldn't trade it for anything.**

- 핵심: `get together`, `the roads are packed`, `bow to their elders`, `wouldn't trade it for anything`

## Q15. 명절에 대한 걱정거리와 대처

**Q:** Talk about some issues or concerns people have about holidays. Why do people have concerns about them, and how do they deal with them?

> **A lot of people feel stressed about holidays**, **and I totally get it.** **The first issue is traffic.** **A trip that normally takes four hours** **can take eight!** **People deal with it by leaving really early**, **or in the middle of the night.** **The second issue is the workload.** **Women, in particular**, **often have to cook all day**, **and that's not fair.** **My mom used to be so exhausted.** **So now, we split the work.** **I do the dishes**, **my relatives share the cooking**, **and we order a few dishes** **from a restaurant.** **The third issue is money**, **because of gifts and cash for kids.** **And let's be honest, some people hate the nosy questions**, **like "When are you getting a promotion?"** **To avoid all that**, **some families just travel overseas** **during the holidays.** **Anyway**, **I think the key is sharing the load**, **so everyone can enjoy it.**

- 핵심: `I totally get it`, `deal with it by -ing`, `split the work`, `nosy questions`, `share the load`
