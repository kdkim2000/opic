## Q1. 자기소개

**Q:** Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

> Hi, how's it going? I'm **a big coffee lover**, so I guess I'll start there. **I can't really start my day without a cup of coffee**, and this morning was no different. I had one before I came to take this test, and I'm **already looking forward to** another one once it's over. I work **in IT**, and my days are pretty busy. I live in an apartment, and I'm **pretty much a homebody**. In my free time, I like listening to music, playing computer games, and reading. So, yeah, that's me **in a nutshell**: **coffee, music, and my computer**. I'm happy to be here, and I hope I do well today!

## Q2. 가장 좋아하는 방 (주방 커피 코너)

**Q:** I'd like to talk about where you live. Tell me about your favorite room in your home. What does it look like? Why do you like that room?

> Well, **if I had to choose, my favorite room is the kitchen.** **I know that sounds a little weird**, **but let me explain.** **In the corner by the window, I set up my own little coffee station.** **I have a hand grinder**, **a drip kettle**, **and a shelf full of coffee beans** **from all over the world.** **The sunlight comes in every morning**, **and it feels like my own mini cafe.** **My friends joke that I spend more time there than anywhere else!** **Every weekend, I grind the beans by hand**, **and the smell fills the whole house.** **That's the moment I feel the most relaxed.** **I like it because it's simple**, **but it makes me really happy.** **For a coffee lover like me**, **it's heaven.**

- 핵심: `If I had to choose`, `set up my own little ~`, `my own mini cafe`, `it's heaven`
- 커피 컨셉의 답변입니다.

## Q3. 집안일 (로봇청소기)

**Q:** Tell me about some housework you do at home. What do you do to keep your house nice and clean?

> **I take care of the housework myself.** **I'm in charge of the dishes**, **the trash**, **and the recycling.** **In Korea, you have to sort the trash carefully**, **so it takes some time.** **I also do the laundry and cook simple meals.** **And I have a secret helper: a robot vacuum!** **I bought it a couple of years ago**, **and it's a lifesaver.** **It cleans the floors every day while I'm at work.** **But it's not perfect.** **One day, it swallowed one of my socks**, **and it started making a horrible noise.** **I had to take the whole thing apart!** **I laughed about it later.** **Now I make sure nothing is on the floor** **before I leave.** **A clean house really makes me feel better.**

- 핵심: `take care of ~`, `sort the trash`, `a lifesaver`, `take it apart`
- 로봇청소기 양말 일화는 지어낸 이야기입니다.

## Q4. 어린 시절 집과 지금 집 비교 (감나무 마당)

**Q:** Tell me about the home you lived in as a child. How was it different from the home you live in now?

> **When I was a kid, I lived in an old house with a big yard.** **The thing I remember most is the persimmon tree** **in the middle of the yard.** **Every fall, it was full of orange fruit**, **and we picked them with a long bamboo pole.** **My mom also dried red peppers** **on a straw mat in the yard**, **so the whole place turned red!** **And of course, with so many people around**, **the house was never quiet.** **In the winter, we gathered around the heated floor** **and ate roasted sweet potatoes.**
>
> **Now, I live in an apartment.** **It's warm, clean, and convenient**, **but I don't have a yard.** **I can't pick fruit from my own tree**, **and kids these days will never know that feeling.** **So, in a way, I miss the old house.** **But I wouldn't trade my warm, modern apartment.** **Both have their good points.**

- 핵심: `The thing I remember most is ~`, `full of ~`, `gather around`, `in a way`, `I wouldn't trade ~`
- 감나무와 마당 풍경 위주로 구성했습니다.

## Q5. 한국의 지형

**Q:** Tell me about the geography of your country. Are there many mountains, rivers, or lakes?

> **Korea is a small country**, **but it has lots of nature.** **About 70 percent of the land is mountains**, **so you can see one almost anywhere.** **The biggest one is Hallasan on Jeju Island**, **and Seoraksan** **is famous for its beautiful fall colors.** **We also have a lot of rivers.** **The Han River runs right through Seoul**, **and people love to picnic and ride bikes** **along it.** **As for lakes, we don't have many big natural ones**, **but there are a lot of man-made lakes** **and dams.** **And since Korea is a peninsula**, **we're surrounded by the sea on three sides.** **Where I live, I can see the ocean from my window.** **To be honest, I think Korea has a little bit of everything.**

- 핵심: `70 percent of the land`, `runs right through ~`, `man-made lakes`, `a peninsula`, `on three sides`

## Q6. 어릴 때 방문한 특별한 장소 (제주 성산일출봉)

**Q:** Tell me about a special place you visited as a child. Maybe it had a unique geographical landmark or feature. What did you see, and what was your impression of that place?

> **One place I'll never forget is Seongsan Ilchulbong on Jeju Island.** **My family took a trip there when I was about eleven.** **It's a huge volcanic crater** **that rises right out of the sea.** **We woke up at 4 a.m.**, **which was brutal**, **and climbed up in the dark** **to see the sunrise.** **My family kept pushing me from behind**, **saying, "Hurry up!"** **I was out of breath**, **but when we reached the top**, **I was speechless.** **The sky turned orange and pink**, **and the sea went on forever.** **The crater was like a giant green bowl.** **I remember thinking**, "**Wow, nature is amazing!**" **That's why it's still one of my favorite places.**

- 핵심: `rises right out of ~`, `out of breath`, `speechless`, `go on forever`
- 어릴 때 가족 여행은 임의 설정입니다.

## Q7. 국내 자연 체험 (집 근처 산 등산)

**Q:** Can you tell me about your experience visiting a natural place in your country? What activities did you do there? Did you go hiking or enjoy the beach?

> **Not long ago, a friend and I went hiking on a mountain** **near my house.** **To be honest, I'm not much of a hiker.** **I'm more of an indoor guy.** **But my friend said**, "**The leaves look gorgeous right now!**" **So I gave in.** **The trail was steeper than I expected**, **and I was huffing and puffing** **after just ten minutes.** **My friend was way ahead of me**, **laughing the whole time.** **But when we got to the top**, **I couldn't believe my eyes.** **You could see the blue sea**, **small islands**, **and ships in the distance.** **We sat on a rock**, **drank hot coffee from a thermos**, and **just enjoyed the view.** **I have to admit, it was worth every step.** **Now I want to go back.**

- 핵심: `not much of a hiker`, `give in`, `huffing and puffing`, `couldn't believe my eyes`, `worth every step`
- 운동을 좋아하지 않는 성향과 어울리는 반전 스토리입니다.

## Q8. 건강한 사람 (이웃)

**Q:** Can you tell me about a healthy person you know? What is he or she like? Why do you think that person is healthy?

> **The healthiest person I know is my neighbor.** **She's in her sixties**, **but she has more energy than I do!** **She gets up at 5 every morning** **and goes for a walk up a small mountain** **near her house.** **She says it keeps her young.** **She also eats lots of vegetables**, **and she hardly ever eats fast food.** **When she invites us for dinner**, **the table is full of greens and soup.** **I always joke**, "**Where's the meat?**" **And she goes to bed early**, **by 9:30.** **I think that's why she looks so good.** **She's also a positive person**, **and she smiles a lot.** **Honestly, I think her happy mind** **is part of her secret.** **She's a great role model for me.**

## Q9. 건강을 위해 해 본 것 (점심 후 걷기)

**Q:** Have you ever done something to stay healthy, such as eating healthy food or working out? What did you do, and how did you feel after trying it?

> **Yes, I tried something simple last year.** **I used to sit at my desk all day**, **even during lunch.** **My back started to hurt**, **and I felt tired all the time.** **So I made a small rule**: **take a 20-minute walk after lunch.** **I asked a coworker to join me**, **and we started walking around our building.** **At first, it felt like a chore.** **But after about two weeks**, **I noticed a big difference.** **I didn't get sleepy in the afternoon**, **and my back felt better.** **We also got to chat**, **so it was a nice break.** **Now it's become a habit.** **Even on rainy days**, **we walk inside the building.** **It proves that you don't need a fancy gym**, **just a little effort.**

- 핵심: `made a small rule`, `a chore`, `notice a big difference`, `become a habit`

## Q10. 건강 개선을 위한 시도 (건강검진 후 PT)

**Q:** You might have tried to improve your health, like quitting smoking or drinking. Perhaps you signed up for an exercise program. What did you try, and what was your reason for doing it?

> **A couple of years ago, I got a wake-up call.** **I took my yearly health checkup at work**, **and the doctor said**, "**Your cholesterol and blood pressure are high.**" **He told me I had to change my lifestyle** **or I'd be in trouble.** **That really shook me up.** **I thought about my family**, **and I wanted to stay healthy for them!**
>
> **So I signed up for a personal training program** **at a gym near my house.** **My trainer was a tough guy**, **and he made me work out three times a week.** **I also cut down on late-night snacks.** **After three months, I lost about five kilograms**, **and my numbers went down.** **I felt lighter and had more energy.** **It was hard at first**, **but it was one of the best decisions I've ever made.**

- 핵심: `wake-up call`, `shake someone up`, `sign up for ~`, `cut down on ~`, `one of the best decisions`
- 지어낸 이야기입니다. 직장 건강검진은 누구나 있는 일입니다.

## Q14. 포장·배달 음식점이 건강 메뉴를 내놓는 이유

**Q:** A lot of fast-food, take-out, or delivery restaurants are offering healthy choices these days. Why do you think these kinds of restaurants have made such changes?

> **I think there are a few reasons.** **First of all, people care a lot more about their health these days.** **Fifteen years ago, nobody counted calories.** **Now, everyone checks the label.** **If a restaurant only sells greasy food**, **they'd lose customers.** **Second, there's a lot of competition.** **Anyone can order from a hundred places on a delivery app**, **so restaurants have to stand out.** **A salad or a low-carb option** **is a good way to do that.**
>
> **Also, the younger generation** **loves eating clean and staying fit.** **A young coworker of mine is a good example.** **She always asks**, "**Is there a healthy option?**" **Even the government pushes it**, **by asking restaurants to show calories.** **So in the end, it's good for business.** **I think it's a win-win**: **people eat better**, **and restaurants make money.**

- 핵심: `care a lot more about ~`, `stand out`, `eat clean`, `a win-win`
- 이유 3개: 건강 의식 → 경쟁 → 젊은 세대·정부

## Q15. 포장·배달 음식의 최근 트렌드

**Q:** Tell me about the latest trends related to take-out or delivery food options. What recent changes have you noticed regarding takeout or delivery food?

> **Well, delivery has changed a lot lately.** **The biggest change is that you can get almost anything delivered.** **It used to be just chicken and Chinese food.** **Now, I can order a coffee**, **a salad**, **or even groceries** **with just a few taps.** **Another trend is single-serving meals.** **More people live alone**, **so restaurants offer small sets** **just for one.**
>
> **I've also noticed that delivery is faster than ever.** **Some stores promise to get it to you in 15 minutes!** **And there's more focus on the environment**, **with paper bags** **instead of plastic.** **Oh, and I've even seen robots** **delivering food in apartment buildings.** **Honestly, it feels like the future.** **I use delivery on weekends**, **and I love that it saves time.** **But I do miss the old days** **of eating out at the restaurant.**

- 핵심: `a few taps`, `single-serving`, `faster than ever`, `it feels like the future`
