## Q8. 한국의 명절 (설날과 추석)

**Q:** Tell me about your country's holidays. Where do people usually celebrate those holidays? How do they celebrate them?

> **Korea has a lot of holidays**, **but the two biggest are Seollal and Chuseok.** **Seollal is the Lunar New Year**, **and Chuseok is the harvest festival in the fall.** **On both days**, **people go back to their hometowns** **to see their families.** **That's why the roads are packed!** **A three-hour drive can turn into eight hours.** **On Seollal**, **people wear hanbok**, **bow to their elders**, and **eat rice cake soup.** **The kids get money**, **which they love.** **On Chuseok**, **families make songpyeon**, **which are half-moon rice cakes**, **and they share a big meal.** **There are other holidays too**, **like Children's Day** and **Buddha's Birthday**, **but family gatherings are the heart of it all.**

- 핵심: `go back to their hometowns`, `the roads are packed`, `bow to their elders`, `the heart of it all`
