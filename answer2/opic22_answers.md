## Q2. 약속을 잡는 방식

**Q:** What kinds of appointments do you usually make, and how do you arrange or schedule them?

> Well, **I make a lot of appointments**, but **they're mostly work meetings and doctor visits**. **At work, my calendar is packed**, so **I book meetings through my company's calendar app**. **For things like haircuts or the dentist, I just book online**, because **I hate talking on the phone**. And **with friends, I send a quick text**, like **"You free on Saturday?"** Honestly, **the thing is, I have a terrible memory**. So **my family and I share one calendar on our phones**. **If it's not on the calendar, it doesn't exist**! **I get a reminder an hour before**, **and that's saved me more than once**. **It's simple, but it works for me.**

- 핵심: `book online`, `the thing is, ~`, `If it's not on the calendar, it doesn't exist`, `saved me more than once`

## Q3. 어린 시절의 약속 (독감 주사)

**Q:** Can you tell me about an appointment you had when you were a child? Maybe it was a doctor's or dentist's appointment. Tell me about your experience with an appointment from your childhood.

> Oh, **I remember this one really well.** **When I was about seven, I had an appointment to get a flu shot** at **the neighborhood clinic**. **I was scared to death of needles.** **My mom was busy, so my aunt took me.** **On the way there, I kept saying, "I feel fine! I don't need it!"** **She just laughed and held my hand tight.** **In the waiting room, I saw a kid crying**, **and that made it even worse.** **When it was my turn, I closed my eyes and squeezed my aunt's hand as hard as I could.** **And guess what? It was over in two seconds!** **The doctor gave me a sticker**, and **my aunt bought me an ice cream on the way home.** **To make a long story short, it wasn't scary at all**, and **I felt kind of silly afterward.**

- 핵심: `scared to death of ~`, `held my hand tight`, `And guess what?`, `To make a long story short`, `felt kind of silly`
