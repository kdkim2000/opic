**Q:** You indicated in the survey that you listen to music. What kind of music do you listen to, and who is your favorite musician or composer?

## Q5. 한국의 교통수단

**Q:** Describe transportation in your country. How do people usually get around, and what modes of transportation are available?

> **Korea has really great public transportation.** **In Seoul, the subway goes almost everywhere**, **and you can transfer to a bus for almost nothing.** **You just tap your card.** **For long trips, there's the KTX**, **our high-speed train.** **You can go from Seoul to Busan in about two and a half hours.** **Taxis are cheap and easy to catch**, and **you can call one on your phone.** **Of course, a lot of people drive too.** **In smaller cities, it's a little different.** **Most people have a car**, and **in some places, ferries go out to the small islands.** **I'd say Korea is one of the most convenient countries to get around in.**

- 핵심: `get around`, `tap your card`, `almost nothing`, `one of the most convenient ~`

## Q14. 카페를 찾는 이유의 변화

**Q:** How have the reasons for visiting coffee shops changed over the years? What do people usually do at coffee shops? Why do people go to coffee shops other than just drinking coffee?

> **Coffee shops have changed a lot over the years.** **Back in the day, people went there just to drink coffee** and **meet someone.** **It was a place for dates and business talks.** **But these days, it's totally different.** **People go to cafes to study, work on their laptops, or just hang out.** **We even have a word for it**: **"cafe study".** **I see students sitting there for hours** with **one cup of coffee!** **People also go for the dessert**, **the cool interior**, **and to take pictures for social media.** **Some cafes are more like art galleries.** **Honestly, for a lot of people, the coffee is just an excuse.** **They're really paying for the space**, **a place to relax and be by themselves.**

- 핵심: `back in the day`, `hang out`, `an excuse`, `paying for the space`
