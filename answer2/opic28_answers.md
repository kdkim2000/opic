## Q2. 한국의 사계절과 날씨

**Q:** Tell me about the seasons in your country. What is the weather like in each season?

> Well, **Korea has four distinct seasons**, and **each one has its own personality**. **Spring is my favorite.** **It's warm and sunny**, and **the cherry blossoms are everywhere**, but **the yellow dust can be a pain.** **Summer is hot and sticky**, and then **the rainy season hits** and **it pours for weeks.** **I can't live without air conditioning in July!** **Fall is the best time of the year.** **The sky is clear**, **the air is crisp**, and **the leaves turn red and gold.** **Winter is cold and dry**, and **it can drop below freezing**, **though where I live it's a bit milder than in Seoul.** **So, to sum up, I'd say you get a little bit of everything**, and **that's what makes living here fun.**

- 핵심: `four distinct seasons`, `pours`, `crisp`, `drop below freezing`, `to sum up`

## Q4. 어릴 때 날씨와 지금 날씨

**Q:** How has the weather changed over the years? What was the weather like when you were a child, and how is it different from the weather now?

> **Looking back, the weather has changed a lot.** **When I was a kid, winters were really cold and snowy.** **I remember waking up to a yard full of snow.** **My friends and I would run outside** and **build a huge snowman.** **My mom would yell**, "**Come in before you freeze!**" **but we never listened.** **The pond near our house froze solid**, **so we even slid on the ice.** **These days, it's totally different.** **Winters are warmer**, **and it hardly snows in my city anymore.** **On the other hand, summers have gotten brutal**, **with heat waves and crazy rain.** **I think it's because of global warming.** **I honestly miss those snowy winters.**

- 핵심: `Looking back`, `froze solid`, `hardly ~ anymore`, `heat waves`, `brutal`

## Q7. 집에서 겪은 문제 (세탁기 누수)

**Q:** Tell me about some problems or issues you've had in your home. Maybe something broke, or someone you lived with didn't cooperate with you. Explain those problems in detail.

> **There's one problem I'll never forget.** **It happened last summer on a Sunday morning.** **I was sipping coffee on the couch** when **my doorbell rang like crazy.** **It was my downstairs neighbor**, **and he said**, "**Water is dripping from my ceiling!**" **I ran to the laundry room**, **and it was a total mess.** **The washing machine hose had come loose**, **and water was flooding the floor.** **I turned off the water valve** and **grabbed every towel in the house.** **I mopped for an hour.**
>
> **Then I went downstairs to apologize.** **His ceiling had a big wet stain**, **and I felt awful.** **I called a repairman** and **paid for the damage.** **To make a long story short**, **it cost me a small fortune**, **but my neighbor was really nice about it.** **Now I check that hose every month!**

## Q8. 한국 사람들이 좋아하는 해외 여행지 (일본)

> **If I had to pick one, I'd say Japan.** **It's super popular with Koreans**, **and there are a few reasons why.** **First of all, it's really close.** **From Busan, it takes about an hour by plane**, **so you can go for a weekend.** **Second, it's easy on the wallet** **compared to Europe.** **And third, the food is amazing**: **ramen, sushi, and all kinds of street food.** **People also love the shopping**, **especially in Osaka and Fukuoka.** **I'd say the best part is that it feels safe and clean.** **You don't have to worry about much**, **even if you don't speak Japanese.** **So, it's no wonder that so many people head there** **every holiday.**

## Q13. 중요한 약속을 취소한 경험 (태풍)

**Q:** That's the end of the situation. Have you ever been in a situation where you had to cancel an important appointment or plan? What happened? When was it, and how did you resolve it?

## Q14. 작은 마을 vs 대도시의 모임과 축하

**Q:** How are gatherings and celebrations in small towns different from those in big cities? Compare celebrations in small towns with those in big cities in your country. How are they different?

> **I'd say they're pretty different.** **In small towns like mine**, **everybody knows everybody.** **So when there's a wedding or a 60th birthday party**, **the whole neighborhood shows up.** **People gather at a big hall** or **even the village square**, **and the neighbors cook and bring food.** **It's loud, warm, and a little bit chaotic.** **Also, older people often lead the party**, **and there's a lot of singing and dancing.**
>
> **In big cities like Seoul, it's more low-key.** **People usually invite just close friends and family.** **They book a nice restaurant** or **rent a party room**, **and everything is planned and quick.** **People are also busy**, **so they often skip the big party.** **Personally, I like the small-town way better.** **It feels more like a real celebration.**

- 핵심: `everybody knows everybody`, `show up`, `low-key`, `the whole neighborhood`
- 두 문단 구조: 작은 마을 → 대도시 → 내 의견

## Q15. 모임 계획의 어려움과 해결법

**Q:** It's sometimes difficult to plan gatherings. Tell me about some challenges related to making plans for gatherings. How do people deal with these problems?

> **Honestly, planning a get-together is harder than it looks.** **The biggest problem is finding a time that works for everyone.** **Last year, I tried to organize a reunion with my old college friends.** **There were ten of us**, **and everyone had a different schedule.** **Some had kids**, **some had business trips**, and **some lived far away.** **The group chat blew up with messages**, **and nobody could agree on anything.** **Then there's the problem of picking a place**, **since everyone likes different food.**
>
> **To solve it, I made a simple online poll** **with three dates.** **People voted**, **and we picked the date with the most votes.** **I also picked a restaurant in the middle of town**, **so it was easy for everybody.** **In the end, nine out of ten showed up.** **I learned that you can't please everyone**, **but a clear poll helps a lot.**

- 핵심: `get-together`, `a time that works for everyone`, `blew up with messages`, `you can't please everyone`
- 문제(시간·장소) → 해결(투표) 구조입니다. 동창 모임은 지어낸 이야기입니다.
