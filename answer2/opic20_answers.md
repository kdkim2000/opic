## Q2. 우리 집 설명

**Q:** Let's talk about where you live. Could you describe your home? What does it look like, and how many rooms does it have?

> **Well, I live in an apartment in a small city by the sea.** **It isn't fancy**, **but it's comfortable.** **We have three bedrooms.** **One is the master bedroom**, **one is a guest room**, and **the third is my own little room**, with **my computer**. **Besides that, there's a living room**, **a kitchen**, and **two bathrooms.** **But my favorite spot is the balcony.** **We put a small table and two chairs there**, **and I call it my "coffee corner".** **On a nice morning, I sit there with a hot coffee** and **watch the sun come up over the sea.** **You can't beat that.**

## Q3. 집에서 하는 일과 (평일 vs 주말)

**Q:** What do you usually do at home? How does your routine differ on weekdays compared to weekends? Tell me about your typical day.

> **Weekdays are a blur for me.** **I wake up around 6**, **make a big cup of coffee**, and **grab a quick bite.** **Then I'm out the door by 7:30.** **When I get back, I'm usually wiped out.** **I eat dinner with my family**, and **if I still have energy left**, **I play a game or read for a bit.** **Then it's lights out by 11.**
>
> **Weekends are a whole different story.** **I sleep in until 8**, **which feels like a luxury!** **Then I grind some fresh coffee beans**, **and the whole house smells amazing.** **After brunch, I might go to the market with a friend** or **just hang around the house.** **In the evening, I like to cook something simple**, **like pasta**, **and watch a movie.** **I'm not a "go out" kind of guy.** **I'm happiest at home, to be honest.**

- 핵심: `a blur`, `wiped out`, `sleep in`, `a whole different story`, `hang around the house`

## Q4. 어린 시절 집과 지금 집 비교

**Q:** Tell me about the home you lived in as a child. How is it different from the home you live in now?

> **I grew up in an old house with a yard.** **The thing I remember best is the persimmon tree** in the yard. **Every fall, it was full of orange fruit**, and **my friends and I would pick them with a long stick.** **I was the smallest kid, so I always got the leftovers!** **The house itself wasn't big**, **and we all slept in one room.** **In the winter, the floor was warm, but the air was freezing.** **We'd all pile onto one blanket**. **It was cozy, but crowded.**
>
> **Now, it's a whole new world.** **My apartment is warm in every room**, **and I even have a little space to myself.** **It's a lot more convenient.** **The thing is, I miss the persimmon tree.** **Nobody gives you free fruit in an apartment!** **I guess you can't have everything.**

- 핵심: `the thing I remember best`, `pile onto ~`, `a whole new world`, `the thing is`

## Q8. 한국의 모임과 축하 행사

**Q:** Tell me about the gatherings and celebrations where you live. What are they like?

> **Koreans love to get together.** **We have big holidays like Seollal and Chuseok**, **when the whole family gathers.** **We eat tons of food**, **play traditional games**, **and the kids get pocket money.** **We also celebrate every milestone.** **A baby's first birthday**, **called "dol"**, **is a big deal.** **Weddings are big too**, **and friends give money in an envelope.** **And then there's the company dinner**, **called "hoesik".** **After work, we have grilled meat**, **a few drinks**, **and sometimes karaoke.** **Here in my city**, **a lot of gatherings take place at seafood restaurants.** **What I like about our gatherings is the food and the warm feeling.** **Nobody's left out.**

- 핵심: `get together`, `a big deal`, `a milestone`, `Nobody's left out`

## Q10. 모임에서 예상 밖 경험 (친구 할아버지 80세 생신)

## Q14. 건강을 중시하는 식당 메뉴의 변화

**Q:** Many restaurants are changing their menus to appeal to health-conscious customers. Tell me about some of the changes restaurants are making.

> **More and more people care about their health these days**, **so restaurants have been changing their menus.** **The first thing I noticed is more salads and salad bowls.** **Even burger places have a salad option now.** **Many restaurants also show calories on the menu**, **so you know what you're eating.** **Another big change is less sugar and less salt.** **Some cafes offer zero-sugar drinks**, **or let you choose how sweet you want it.** **And you can find plant-based food too**, **like vegetable burgers or tofu dishes.** **Restaurants even use brown rice instead of white rice.** **I personally think it's a good trend.** **Honestly, I need it myself**, **because I love ramyun and fried chicken!** **So I'm glad to have healthier choices.**

- 핵심: `health-conscious`, `calories`, `plant-based`, `a good trend`

## Q15. 체인점 vs 동네 식당 경험

>
> **A local restaurant is a different story.** **Near my office, there's a small seafood place** **run by an older couple.** **There's no menu on the wall.** **The owner just says, "Today we have grilled mackerel."** **The first time I went, I was confused**, **but I trusted her.** **It turned out to be the best fish I'd ever had.** **It came with ten side dishes**, **and she kept giving me more rice!** **It felt like eating at my mom's house.** **So for convenience, I'd pick a chain.** **But for a real experience, I'd go local every time.**
