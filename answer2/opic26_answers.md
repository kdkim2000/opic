## Q2. 가장 좋아하는 해변

**Q:** Tell me about your favorite beach. What does it look like? Where is it, and how often do you go there?

> Well, **my favorite beach is a pebble beach** **near my home.** **It's only about 20 minutes from my place by car.** **It's not your typical sandy beach.** **Instead of sand, it's covered with smooth, round, black pebbles.** **That's what makes it special!** **When the waves come in and out, the stones make this soft, rolling sound.** **It's so relaxing**, **it's like a natural lullaby.** **Behind the beach, there's a row of old pine trees** **and a few small cafes.** **I go there once or twice a month**, **usually on weekends.** **To be honest, I go there more than anywhere else.** **Whenever I feel stressed out from work**, **that sound just washes it all away.**

- 핵심: `your typical ~`, `a natural lullaby`, `stressed out`, `wash it all away`

## Q7. 문제 하나 자세히 (욕실 천장 누수)

**Q:** Pick one of the problems you mentioned above and describe it in detail. What was the problem? What caused it, and how did you handle it?

> **The water leak is the one I remember best.** **It happened last summer.** **One morning, I walked into the bathroom** **and saw a big brown stain on the ceiling.** **Then, drip, drip, drip!** **Water was coming down.** **I thought, "Oh no, this is going to be a nightmare."** **I went upstairs and talked to my neighbor.** **It turned out that a pipe in their bathroom was cracked.** **Luckily, they were really nice about it.** **They called a plumber right away**, **and the building's insurance covered the repairs.** **It took about a week to fix.** **The plumber fixed the pipe** **and repainted our ceiling.** **I learned two things.** **First, stay calm.** **Second, be nice to your neighbors**, **because you never know when you'll need them!**
