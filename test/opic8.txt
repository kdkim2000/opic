Opic8 — 문제 스크립트
※ 녹취를 바탕으로 정리. 반복 질문은 1회만 수록.
※ (*) 표시 = 녹취가 불완전하여 문맥에 맞춰 보완한 부분

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[의류 / 패션 (돌발)]
Q2. What kind of clothes do people wear in your country? What are some fashion trends or (*) special features of clothing in your country?

Q3. What clothes do you like to wear? What do you usually wear? What are you wearing now?

Q4. Fashion trends change all the time. What kinds of clothes were popular when you were younger, and what kinds of clothes are popular now?

------------------------------------------------------------
[공원 가기]
Q5. Please describe the park you often go to. (*) Where is it, and what does it look like?

Q6. How did you first become interested in going to parks? How has your interest in going to parks changed over time?

Q7. Tell me about one memorable experience you had at a park. What happened, and why was it memorable?

------------------------------------------------------------
[호텔 (돌발)]
Q8. Tell me about hotels in your country. What are they like? Where are they located?

Q9. Can you tell me about the last time you stayed at a hotel? Where did you stay, and what did you do at the hotel?

Q10. Tell me about a memorable hotel you stayed at. Which hotel was it, and why was that hotel special to you?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 친구 생일파티]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You've been invited to your friend's birthday party, and the party is going to be held at a bar. Call your friend and ask three or four questions about the location of the party.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there is a problem I want you to resolve. You have to take a test tomorrow, so you cannot make it to your friend's birthday party. Call your friend, explain the situation, and give two or three alternatives.

Q13. Have you ever been in a situation where you had to cancel or change a plan because something came up? What was the situation, and how did you solve the problem?

------------------------------------------------------------
[해외여행]
Q14. You indicated in the survey that you take vacations internationally. How has traveling abroad changed over the years? Is it easier or more difficult to travel compared to the past?

Q15. Tell me about the things that are important to people when they travel overseas. What are the things people are interested in seeing? What are the things that are important for travelers to do?
