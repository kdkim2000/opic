여우오픽 모의고사 4 (난이도 5~6) — 문제 스크립트
※ 녹취를 바탕으로 정리. 반복 질문은 1회만 수록.
※ (*) 표시 = 녹취가 불완전하여 문맥에 맞춰 보완한 부분

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[교통수단 (돌발)]
Q2. Tell me about transportation in your country. What types of transportation do people use?

Q3. What kind of transportation do you use to get around? Do you use public transportation, or do you drive?

Q4. Sometimes riding a subway or bus can be uncomfortable. Have you ever had any problems while taking public transportation?

------------------------------------------------------------
[캠핑]
Q5. Where do you enjoy going camping? Describe the place where you often go camping in as much detail as you can.

Q6. (*) When did you first go camping? How did you become interested in camping?

Q7. Tell me about your most memorable camping trip. Where did you go? What did you do during your camping trip? Why was it so memorable?

------------------------------------------------------------
[여가 시간 (돌발)]
Q8. I'd like to know what people in your country do in their free time. What activities do people do, and how do they spend their free time?

Q9. How have the ways you spend your free time changed over the years? What did you do when you had free time in the past, and what do you do when you have free time now?

Q10. Tell me about the last time you had free time. When was it, and what did you do? How did you spend the time?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 여행]
Q11. (*) I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You want to go on a trip. Call a travel agency and ask three or four questions about the trip you want to take.

Q12. (*) I'm sorry, but there is a problem I want you to resolve. You made a reservation for a trip, but something came up, so you can't go. You have to change your flight schedule. Call the travel agency, explain the situation, and give two or three alternatives.

Q13. Have you ever had a problem while planning for a trip? What was the problem, and how did you solve the problem?

------------------------------------------------------------
[영화 보기]
Q14. Compare the movies today to the movies you watched when you were a child. How have movies changed over the years? Tell me the similarities and differences between them.

Q15. What issues about movies do you talk about with your friends or family members these days? What are they, and why are they big issues?
