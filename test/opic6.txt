여우오픽 모의고사 6 (난이도 5~6) — 문제 스크립트
※ 녹취를 바탕으로 정리. 반복 질문은 1회만 수록.
※ (*) 표시 = 녹취가 불완전하여 문맥에 맞춰 보완한 부분

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[산업 / 회사 (돌발)]
Q2. Tell me about a rising industry or company in your country. What is it, and why is that industry or company rising and becoming popular?

Q3. Tell me about a promising company in the industry you mentioned above. Why is the company promising? What benefits does it bring to your society?

Q4. Tell me about recent changes in the company you mentioned above. For example, the company released a new product. (*) What changes have there been, and how have they affected the company?

------------------------------------------------------------
[콘서트 보기]
Q5. What kind of concerts do you like to go to? Why do you like this kind of concert?

Q6. When did you start going to concerts? How did you first become interested in going to concerts?

Q7. Tell me about the last time you went to a concert. Whose concert was it? Where was it, and who did you go with?

------------------------------------------------------------
[국내여행]
Q8. You indicated in the survey that you enjoy traveling domestically. What is your favorite location, and why do you like that place?

Q9. Tell me about a trip you took in your youth. Where did you go, and who did you go with? Why did you go there?

Q10. Have you had any interesting or unforgettable experiences while you were traveling in your own country? Tell me what happened in detail.

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 병원 예약]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You want to make a doctor's appointment. Call the doctor's office and ask three or four questions about making an appointment.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there is a problem I want you to resolve. You made an appointment with your doctor, but something came up, so you cannot make it. Call the doctor's office, explain the situation, and reschedule the appointment.

Q13. Tell me about an experience when you had to cancel an appointment you made. What happened? Why did you have to cancel the appointment, and how did you deal with the situation?

------------------------------------------------------------
[지형 / 비슷한 나라 (돌발)]
Q14. Tell me about a country that has similar geographical features to your country. What changes has that country had in recent years?

Q15. Tell me about an article you read about the country you mentioned above. Was it political or economic news about the country? What was the news about?
