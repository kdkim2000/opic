[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[음악 감상]
Q2. You indicated in the survey that you listen to music. What kind of music do you like listening to? Who is your favorite singer or composer?

Q3. (*) When and where do you usually listen to music? Do you listen to music on the radio, or do you go to concerts?

Q4. (*) When did you first become interested in music? How has your interest in music changed over time? How is the music you liked as a child different from the music you like now?

------------------------------------------------------------
[해변 가기]
Q5. Tell me about a beach you like to go to. Where is it? What does it look like? (*) How often do you go there?

Q6. Tell me about the beach that you recently went to. What did it look like? What did you like about the beach?

Q7. Tell me about a memorable trip to the beach. Why did you go? What did you do there? What made it so memorable?

------------------------------------------------------------
[모임·축하 (돌발)]
Q8. Tell me about gatherings and celebrations in your country. How do people celebrate them, and what do they do when they get together?

Q9. Tell me about the last gathering or celebration you went to. What was it, and what did you do there?

Q10. Tell me about a memorable experience you had at a gathering or celebration. What happened, and why was it unforgettable?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 가구]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You want to buy a piece of furniture for your house. Ask the salesperson at the furniture store three or four questions about the furniture you want to buy.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there is a problem I'd like you to resolve. You just found out that there is a problem with the furniture you bought. Call the store, explain the situation, and suggest two or three solutions.

Q13. (*) Have you ever had a problem with something you bought? What was the problem, and how did you resolve it?

------------------------------------------------------------
[가족·친구]
Q14. (*) Choose two people among your family or friends and compare them. How are they similar, and how are they different?

Q15. What do you usually talk about with your friends or family members? Tell me about the last conversation you had with one of them.
