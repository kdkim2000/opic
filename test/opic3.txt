여우오픽 모의고사 3 (난이도 5~6) — 문제 스크립트
※ 녹취를 바탕으로 정리. 반복 질문은 1회만 수록.
※ (*) 표시 = 녹취가 불완전하여 문맥에 맞춰 보완한 부분

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[집 / 거주]
Q2. Describe your house to me. What does it look like? What types of rooms does it have? Tell me about your favorite room.

Q3. What activities do you usually do when you are at home? What do you do on weekdays, and what do you do on weekends?

Q4. Tell me about the house you lived in when you were a child. How was it different from the house you are living in now?

------------------------------------------------------------
[가족·친구 (돌발)]
Q5. Tell me about one of your family members or friends. What is he or she like? Tell me about the person in detail.

------------------------------------------------------------
[약속 (돌발)]
Q6. What kind of appointments do you usually make? Who do you make appointments with, and how do you make them?

Q7. Tell me about an appointment you made when you were a child. What was the appointment for? Was it with a doctor or a dentist? How did it go?

Q8. Tell me about a memorable experience you had during an appointment. Was it unexpected? What happened, and how did you deal with the situation?

------------------------------------------------------------
[해외여행]
Q9. Tell me about your first trip abroad. Where did you go, and who did you go with? What did you do there?

Q10. Tell me about a memorable or unforgettable experience you had while traveling abroad. What happened, and why was the experience memorable?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 공원]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. Your friend wants to go to the park this weekend and invited you to go together. Call your friend and ask three or four questions about your friend's plan.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there is a problem I want you to resolve. You were supposed to meet your friend to go to the park together, but something came up, so you cannot make it. Call your friend, explain the situation, and suggest two or three alternatives.

Q13. Tell me about a memorable experience you had at a park. What happened, and why was it memorable to you? Also, how did you deal with the situation?

------------------------------------------------------------
[인터넷 (돌발)]
Q14. Tell me about some issues people are concerned about related to the Internet these days. Maybe there are security or safety-related issues. How do they affect people's lives?

Q15. How is using the Internet different among people in different age groups? What do younger people normally do on the Internet, and what do (*) older people do on the Internet?
