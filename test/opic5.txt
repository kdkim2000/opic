여우오픽 모의고사 5 (난이도 5~6) — 문제 스크립트
※ 녹취를 바탕으로 정리. 반복 질문은 1회만 수록.
※ (*) 표시 = 녹취가 불완전하거나 누락되어 문맥에 맞춰 보완한 부분

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[은행 (돌발)]
Q2. Tell me about banks in your country. Where are they located, and what do they look like?

Q3. What do you do when you go to the bank? Tell me what you typically do when you go to the bank in as much detail as you can.

Q4. How have banks changed over the years? How were banks when you were young, and how are they now?

------------------------------------------------------------
[지형 / 야외활동 (돌발)]
Q5. Tell me about your country's geography. Are there many mountains, rivers, or lakes in your country?

Q6. (*) What are some typical outdoor activities that people in your country do in those places? What do people usually do there?

Q7. Tell me about a special landscape you have seen. What was special about it? When did you see it, and what did you do there?

------------------------------------------------------------
[카페 / 커피전문점]
Q8. Tell me about cafes or coffee shops in your country. What are they like? What do people usually do there?

Q9. Tell me about the first time you went to a coffee shop. When was it, and who did you go with? What do you remember the most about it?

Q10. Tell me about the most memorable event you had when you were at a cafe. What happened, and what made it so memorable to you?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 친척 집 봐주기]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. Your relatives are going to travel, and their house is going to be empty. You are asked to watch the house while they are gone on the trip. Call your relatives and ask three or four questions about what you need to do.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there is a problem I need you to resolve. You just arrived at your relatives' house, but you cannot get into the house because you cannot find the keys to the house. Call your relatives and ask them (*) two or three questions to solve the problem.

Q13. That's the end of the situation. Have you ever been in a situation where you couldn't keep a promise to a friend or a family member? What was the reason you couldn't keep the promise, and how did you deal with the situation?

------------------------------------------------------------
[음악 기기 (음악 감상)]
Q14. Tell me about some music gadgets or equipment people in your country are interested in. Why are people interested in these music gadgets or equipment?

Q15. (*) [녹취 누락 — 같은 주제의 예상 문항]
Tell me about a problem you had with a music gadget or equipment. What happened, and how did you resolve the problem?
