Opic7 — 문제 스크립트 (영상: 여우오픽 모의고사 8, 난이도 5~6)
※ 녹취를 바탕으로 정리. 반복 질문은 1회만 수록.
※ (*) 표시 = 녹취가 불완전하여 문맥에 맞춰 보완한 부분

------------------------------------------------------------
[자기소개]
Q1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
[재활용 (돌발)]
Q2. Tell me about recycling in your country. How do people recycle, and what do they recycle?

Q3. How do you recycle? Do you recycle every day? Where do you take the recyclable items? Tell me all the steps you take for recycling.

Q4. Tell me about a memorable or unexpected incident you experienced while you were recycling. What happened, and why was it memorable to you?

------------------------------------------------------------
[휴일 / 명절 (돌발)]
Q5. Let's talk about holidays in your country. What holidays are there, and how are they celebrated?

Q6. (*) Tell me about a holiday experience you had when you were young. What did you do, and who did you spend the holiday with?

Q7. (*) Tell me about the last holiday you had. What did you do? Was there anything special about that holiday?

------------------------------------------------------------
[영화 보기]
Q8. What is your favorite kind of movie? Why do you like to watch that type of movie?

Q9. Tell me about the last time you went to a movie. Who did you go with? What did you do before and after going to the movie?

Q10. Tell me about the most memorable movie you have seen. What was it about? How did you feel while watching it?

------------------------------------------------------------
[롤플레이 — 해변]
Q11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You want to go to the beach with your friend. Call your friend and ask three or four questions to make plans to go to the beach.

Q12. I'm sorry, but there is a problem I want you to resolve. You are supposed to go to the beach with your friend, but you just heard that the weather will be really bad that day. Call your friend, explain the situation, and give two or three solutions.

Q13. Have you ever had a problem or an unexpected event at the beach? What was the situation, and how did you handle it?

------------------------------------------------------------
[집 / 거주]
Q14. I'd like to know about where you live. How do you solve problems when something happens in your house? How are the ways you solve problems at your home different from the ways your parents solve problems at their home?

Q15. Tell me about issues people have when they rent a house or an apartment. What are the things people are concerned about when renting a house or an apartment, and how do they deal with them?
